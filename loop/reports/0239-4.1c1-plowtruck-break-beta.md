## Findings
1. [POLISH] A centred rock/log hit at 8 m/s with the blade down stops the truck dead (21.0 m, 0.18 m/s) instead of "keeps 60% of its speed" (EXPANSION 4.1b2b). The pickup with the Fjellskjær does the same in the same test (21.3 m, 0.34 m/s), so it is not plow-truck specific and not a 4.1c1 regression: my rock (r 0.7, top +0.8 m) probably meets the body hull as well as the blade edge. Worth one look at whether a 9.8 t truck being stopped by a 0.7 m rock is intended; a rock only the edge reaches was not tested.
2. [POLISH] The blade's down/up is not saved: after a reload with it down, the truck comes back with it up (hint "E: drop the blade"). Probably intended (the up/down is session state); mention only in case a plow driver would expect it to persist.
3. [POLISH, scenario note] My first run in natural 54 cm snow made the truck stall at 12 m with the blade down at 8 m/s start: it is no "run into a rock" test without a cleared strip. Not a game bug.
