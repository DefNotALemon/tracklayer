# 0098: 2.1b on-foot "use" (E / L3 / touch verb button): beta break test (main d6bb838, v177)

Focus: slice 2.1b (landed v169, fewest reports: none; nothing new on main since 0097). Reran `x2_use.mjs` path via the new
`x2_usebreak.mjs` (committed): ~25 checks over out-of-order use. One finding, no BLOCKER. Page errors: none.
Still: `~/.hermes/cache/scratch/tlloop/reports/0098.jpg` (after-reload frame only; the findings are state, not visual).

## What held
- Riding + bogged: `footUse()` null, E and pad L3 do not dig, no touch button.
- On foot, digging, walk 6 m away: `footUse()` null, hint switches to "Sled 7 m ahead ... walk back and press Q", dig stops, and E far away does not restart it.
- Mash E x20 (25 ms taps): state stays consistent (bogged, boolean dig), no errors.
- Freed while digging: use null, dig flag not left on for the next bog.
- Dead, or garage open: `footUse()` null and L3 does nothing.
- Device flip mid-dig (keyboard -> touch -> keyboard): touch button reads STOP and hint "tap STOP"; back on keyboard the hint reads "E: stop" at once.
- Riding on touch: use button hidden. Reload while on foot and bogged: back on the sled, not bogged, not digging, no stale state.

## Findings
1. POLISH: with the tablet open (Map app) while on foot and bogged beside the sled, E still starts the dig (hint "Digging 2% · E: stop"),
   i.e. a key meant for the tablet/world acts on the world behind it. Only matters if E is also a tablet key; suggest `footUse()`/`footAction()`
   return null/no-op while `TABLET` is open, as they already do for `GS.garageOpen`.
2. POLISH (test note, not a bug): the first E right after Q-off is ignored until the dismount animation ends (`DMT.on`). That is the intended
   swing; a scenario must poll `DMT.on`, not sleep a fixed time.

Not tested: SAR "HELP UP"/"LOAD"/"RIG TOW" labels under pad/touch, winch HOOK/UNHOOK range edge (2.6 m), two simultaneous verbs.
