# 0365: damaged-save fixes (tlx-vehicles v36, tlx-plow v34, f83cf06 / 27edb9d) (beta, main f83cf06)

New `x5_savesafe_beta.mjs` (all ok, page errors none). The builder's own `x5_savesafe.mjs` also re-run: all ok. No stills: the findings are numbers.

## What held
- Truck fuel `true`, `[]`, `[7]`, `" "`, `"12abc"`, `"0x10"`, `"1e999"` planted in a save and reloaded: every record loads as a number in 0..70 and the cab reads a sane `GS.fuel` after `swapTo`.
- Base-game root `fuel` as `"abc"`, `"0"`, `null`, `{}`: `GS.fuel` is a finite number >= 0 each time.
- Driver record with `p` null, -50, `"abc"`, `"900"`, or `out` with no truck: loads as a clean home record (p 0), no exception over 8 h of game time, truck not left marked out.
- Driver `back` with `p` 1e308: loaded at 1611.7 (the road's end) and home within 8 h, p 0.

## Findings
1. POLISH: `"1e999"` (parses to Infinity) and `"0x10"`-style strings are read as 0 or as the number, not as "full": a junk-but-large fuel string becomes an empty tank, so the player starts the truck empty. Only reachable by hand-edited saves; fine to leave.
2. POLISH: a driver with a bad `p` (null, negative, text) is silently reset to home rather than clamped to the road. Safe and the truck comes back, but any work in progress that trip is lost without a note. Not worth fixing.

No BLOCKER or BUG found. Not tested: pad/touch (no UI change), other vehicle kinds with fuel (boats are lane C's).
