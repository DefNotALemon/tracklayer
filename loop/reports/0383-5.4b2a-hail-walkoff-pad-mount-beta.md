# 0383 — 5.4b2a hail edges: pad, walk-off, mount while hailed (beta)

Run on origin/main c566b61 (v299). Nothing new landed for lane A since 0382, so this goes back to a part with few reports (hail: 0343, 0345, 0355). New scenario `x5_plowdriver_hail_walkoff_beta.mjs` (exit 0, 22 ok, page errors none). Setup: plow truck bought and assigned to a hired driver, out at 10:00, you on foot ~20 m away.

## Results
1. OK — DualSense L3 hails (hail clock 59.3 s) and the control reads "WAVE ON", hint "L3: wave Isak on" (no "E"). A second L3 waves on and clears the clock. The same hint read "E: hail Isak" before the first pad press, so the hint follows the input device.
2. OK — walking away from a hailed truck in 5 m steps: it stays stopped at 18, 23, 28, 33, 38 and 43 m and drives off at 47 m (7.7 m/s on the next sample). That matches hailOff 45 within one step. After that no stale WAVE ON, and it is at 12.5 m/s a second later.
3. OK — hail, then mount the sled 18 m from the truck: no error, you ride, the truck stays stopped and the clock runs down (55 -> 52 s). Riding 110 m off releases it, and it drives at 12.4 m/s.
4. POLISH — while you sit on the sled beside a hailed truck, the hail stays on. It cannot be waved on, because the hail control is on foot only, so a rider has to wait out the 60 s or ride 45 m away. The driver does not react to your riding off either, which is fine. Suggested: release the hail when you mount, or offer WAVE ON from the saddle.
5. NOT TESTED — touch (`#tcE` pill) hail; a hail across a sleep (0343 #5 covers a clock jump); a hail with two drivers' trucks within 30 m.

No BLOCKER or BUG.
