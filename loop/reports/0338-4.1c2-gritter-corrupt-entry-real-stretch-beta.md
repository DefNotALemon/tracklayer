# 0338 — 4.1c2 gritter: corrupt grit entries planted under a REAL stretch key — main @ e304d9b
Scenario: NEW `tools/playtest/scenarios/x4_gritter_realkey_beta.mjs`. Follows 0322's "not covered": plants 10 bad entries under the real key `fv888:0` (Kjøllefjord–Mehamn road, 40 bins), reloads each, parks a plow truck on that stretch, reads `gritAt`/`grip`/cover/hint, toggles the gritter, rolls 6 m/s for ~1 s of wall time. 2 of 51 asserts fail; no page errors, no throws.

## Findings
1. OK — every case boots, `gritAt` stays 0..1, grip 1..1.35, `gritKg` finite, hint is a string, toggle works. Wrong-length arrays (short 1 / long 80) are rebuilt to 40 bins on the first spread, Infinity hour (saves as null) is dropped by the tidy, negative/huge `s0` and a 200,000-entry array behave (array rebuilt to 40 on spread).
2. POLISH — string hours (`"12"`) survive: before spreading the stretch reads 100% gritted (JS coercion), and after spreading bins the truck didn't touch stay strings in the saved table (`fin` false). Same for `"x"`/object bins in "mixed junk". Harmless to play, but corrupt values never heal. Fix: in the tidy (tlx-plow.js ~line 193) rewrite `g.h` as `g.h.map(h => Number.isFinite(h) ? h : 0)` and cap its length at the stretch's bins.
3. POLISH — wrong-LENGTH arrays are trusted for reads until the truck spreads: a planted 80-bin array reports the stretch 100% gritted and grip 1.35 though the real stretch has 40 bins (the length check lives only in `spread`). Add `g.h.length === q.n` to `gritAt`/`gritCover`.
4. POLISH — future hours (99999) pass the tidy (`hour - h < GRT.hours` is true for negative age), so the entry is kept forever though `gritLive` reads it as 0. Tidy should also drop `age < 0`.
