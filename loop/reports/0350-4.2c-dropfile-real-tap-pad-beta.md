# 0350 — 4.2 Plow app DROP / TAKE ON by real touch tap and pad A — main @ 2df028b (no code landed since 0349)

Scenario: NEW `tools/playtest/scenarios/x4_dropfile_realtap_beta.mjs` (touch events via `Input.dispatchTouchEvent`, pad via fake gamepad, lot "hosp", pickup with a blade). Earlier DROP tests (x4_dropfile*, 0259) all called `TLXPL.lot.drop` directly. Three runs; page errors none. No stills (NOSTILLS).

## Findings
1. OK — real tap on TAKE ON takes the lot; real tap on DROP of a snowed-in lot (level 0.48) drops it and files one miss (`lotRec.hosp.miss 1`, until +171 h); button hit-test lands on the right `[data-act]` button; app text has no NaN/undefined; pad A on a focused TAKE ON takes the lot (pad D-pad down reaches it).
2. POLISH — the button flips under the finger: after DROP the TAKE ON button sits at the same spot (876,383 vs DROP at 884,383, 69x31 vs 53x30 px). A second tap on the same spot (double tap, or a tap that registers twice) retakes the lot at once, with the miss from the DROP already on file, and there's no confirm. A real double tap on DROP therefore ends up with a lot the player just gave up, carrying a recorded miss. Suggested fix: ignore taps on a flipped button for ~500 ms, or confirm DROP when the lot is snowed in (that's the one case with a cost).
3. BUG? (flaky, one of three runs) — in one run the TAKE ON button measured 65x27 px (the others 69x31) and a real tap at its centre did NOT take the lot; the next DROP-button lookups then found nothing. The layout of the row changed between runs (height 27 vs 31), so the app can reflow between my measure and the tap, or the first tap after opening the app is swallowed. Not reproduced twice; rerun with the scenario to see. Suggest the builder check whether the Plow app re-renders on a timer (`conRefresh`) while the tablet is open: a re-render under the finger would explain it.
4. POLISH — tap target is 27-31 px tall, under the usual 44 px touch guideline; DROP is 53 px wide. Fine on a tablet screen, tight with a thumb on a phone.
