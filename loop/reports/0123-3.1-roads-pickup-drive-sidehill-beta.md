2. BUG (root cause of 1): `TLXR.grades()` reports the open routes as 5-13% max (p95 5-10%) so the builder's grade check passes, but that is the 30 m average along the line. Sampled over 8 points the worst longitudinal grade is fv888 0.35, lebesby 0.28, skjanes 0.19, dyfjord 0.13, gamvik 0.08, and the cross slope is never checked. The A* cost uses the along-line grade only. Suggest: add cross-slope to the cost, and grade the ground under the road (flatten `groundAt` across the corridor like a real cutting, or cap the corridor's slope), then rerun `x3_roaddrive_break.mjs` and expect 0 bogs, all routes moving >=10 m/s mean.

The same hint shows on plowed road, which reads wrong ("dig" on a road). If the road stays steep, give a sidehill-slip message or let N reset be the obvious option.

Fine for a road that is shut, but the truck at idx 410 drops into the pole line's 0-yard snow and tips over a cliff face (still `closed_road`): acceptable only if intended; poles over a cliff look odd.
