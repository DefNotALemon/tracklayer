# 0288 — 5.4a2 ASSIGN THE ... button on a plow driver's Staff card: pad, touch, narrow panes — main @ ad994db (v255, tlx-staff v7, tlx-plow v22)
Scenarios: NEW `x5_assign_pad_touch_beta.mjs` (two plow trucks, driver hired on the Gamvik road) and `x5_assign_pad_probe.mjs` (prints the d-pad focus path). Headless, numbers only, no stills. No game code landed since 0280 (ba3c932/dfad2fe are the newest), so this covers 0280's "not tested": pad/touch on ASSIGN.

## Findings
No BLOCKER, no BUG.
1. OK — two same-model trucks give two buttons, "ASSIGN THE NORDTREKK BRØYT 4X2 1" / "... 2": distinct labels. Pad A on a focused ASSIGN sets `pdr.truck` to that truck's uid. After assigning, the card shows only "TAKE THE TRUCK BACK" (both ASSIGNs gone); pressing it clears the truck and both ASSIGN buttons come back.
2. OK — touch (SET.touch on, pointerdown/up + click) on the first ASSIGN assigns truck 1. Page errors none. Card text has no undefined/NaN.
3. OK — narrow panes: the buttons stay 124 px wide x 36 px tall at 640x360 and 390x844; pane scrollWidth equals clientWidth (no sideways scroll).
4. POLISH — d-pad focus order on the card is not left-to-right. With the card's default focus (stc:1), pad "right" goes stp → stk → str → **stc** → stx:1:av1 → stx:1:av2: it passes back over the card itself (stc) before reaching the role buttons. "Down" never leaves the row (stp stays stp). Reachable in 5 right presses, but a pad player who presses down looking for ASSIGN stays on PAY/stp. With no focus set, 8 presses (down, then right x7) ended on the header close button `__x`, not on ASSIGN. Suggested fix: give the card's own tf key an order before its buttons, or let "down" step to the next row's first control.
5. NOTE (my scenario) — the first version asserted "down reaches ASSIGN" and failed for that reason; the scenario now keeps that check and it still FAILs (finding 4). Treat the one FAIL in `x5_assign_pad_touch_beta` as POLISH 4, not a regression.
