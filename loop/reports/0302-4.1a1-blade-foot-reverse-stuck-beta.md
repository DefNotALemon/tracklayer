1. **POLISH / possible BUG — the hint's way out does not work from a parked-in-snow start.** Pickup + b23 blade set down by `park()` in 56 cm of settled snow (fresh 0.005): "The wheels are digging in. Ease off, or back out along your own ruts." / `WHEELS DIGGING IN · ease off the throttle` show under W, and the blade-down hint says "back up to cleared ground and plow in from there". But holding S for 4 game s moved the truck 0.01 m, with the blade UP as well as down (`GB.dir` went -1, speed 0.002), and W for 5 s afterwards moved 0.01 m. There are no ruts at a teleport so this may be a test artefact (0301 #4 saw the same on open flat snow), but a player who gets the truck into 55+ cm uncut snow (e.g. a sled-to-truck swap in the backcountry, or a storm settling on a parked truck) has two hints that name an exit that did not move it. Check: can a wheeled truck with no ruts ever reverse out of 0.5 m settled snow? If not, the toast needs a real option (shovel / dig, or the 3.3 DIG).
2. **BUG (intermittent, unconfirmed) — Q did not get out of the truck.** Same stuck start with blade down: run 1 Q put me on foot within 10 s wall; run 2 (identical setup, night/day differed only by nothing) `FOOT.on` stayed false after 10 s while the truck was digging in. Could be the 2.3 door sequence refusing while bogged, with no message. If intended, say so in a toast.
3. OK — with the rider on foot the blade stays `down` on the parked truck (state kept), no cut counted while walking (`m` stayed 0), the ride hint is empty on foot.
4. NOT TESTED — second truck swap with the blade down on the first: `TLXV.buy("tractor")` returned nothing and nothing was added (no Freight licence / cash path in my scenario); the owned list stayed one truck. Left for a rerun with the licence set.
5. NOT TESTED — night cut counting (`m` rises) failed only because of #1 (the truck never moved), not evidence either way.
