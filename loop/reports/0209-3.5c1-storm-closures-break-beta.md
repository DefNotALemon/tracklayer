## Findings
1. [Verified] The two roads are independent: both snowed in -> both shut; plowing fv888 leaves Lebesby shut and puts fv888 to "wait".
2. [Verified] Option "Road traffic/convoy" switched off with two cars queued at a lowered Lebesby boom: both cars were driving again within 25 game s (head car 845 m -> 998 m, v 6.8 m/s), booms up, "OPEN" boards. Switched on again: the closure is back (road still snowed in -> "shut"). No tick error.
2b. [Verified] Night: a shut road reads "CLOSED / storm", lamps on, booms down. Reload with the road snowed in at night keeps it shut, booms down (up 0), no convoy error.
3. [POLISH] Reload skips the wait. Road plowed and waiting for the 50:00 convoy (st `wait`, at 50), then page reload: the road comes back `open`, boards "OPEN", booms up, `at` 0. Road snow is saved but the convoy clock is not, so a player who reloads (or quits and resumes) walks round the closure. Suggest: on load, a plowed road that is within the gates' window of a closure... simplest: save `{st, at}` per road in TLX state, or accept and document it as a design choice (the boom does not stop a sled anyway, so low impact).
4. [Note] Storm at convoy time cannot be tested without the plow truck: the "no convoy in a storm, next hour" branch is only reachable when `shut` is still true in state `wait`, which needs a plow truck that has not cleared the road. With the truck off a plowed road with nobody queued simply opens at convoy time. Not a bug, but the storm delay is covered by 0208's slice scenario only.
5. [Inconclusive] My first run's `reload()` (done right after setting `WX.at = () => 1; GS.storm = 1`) printed "BOOT FAILED: title menu never appeared", page errors none. The same reload with no storm was fine. Possibly the harness (storm state saved then title blocked), not the game; not reproduced, not chased.
