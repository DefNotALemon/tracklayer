# 0113: 2.7 wear and repair (garage Trucks tab): beta test (main 92400f8, v186)

Focus: nothing new landed since 0112, so the landed part with the fewest reports: 2.7's repair service (no report touched it). Wrote `x2_repairbreak.mjs` (committed): 32 asserts, 31 pass, 1 FAIL. Page errors: none. No stills (logic only). No BLOCKER.

## What held
- 40 m rule: truck at 41 m from the garage = row locked, clicking it changes nothing; 39 m = offered ($1400 for a 30% Fjellvogn).
- Cash one short ($1395 vs $1400) = locked, click repairs nothing; exactly $1400 = repaired to 100%, cash 0, row disappears.
- Prices: 99.5%+ no row; 99.4% truck $10, trailer $5; 99% $20; 0% $2000.
- Truck being driven: offered at 20 m, repaired (cond 100, runtime reads 100), locked at 60 m.
- Saved cond NaN / -50 / 250 / "abc" / null: all load clamped to 0-100, garage rows have no NaN or undefined.

## Findings
1. POLISH: `repair()` has no "already at 100%" guard. Three clicks on the same (stale) row charged $810 for an $800 repair: the 2nd and 3rd calls hit `repairCost` min `$5` and take $5 each for nothing. Real fingers can't click a row that `garageRefresh` already replaced, so this is only reachable by a script or a lag-held touch tap, hence POLISH. Fix: `if (o.cond >= 99.5) return;` at the top of `repair`.
2. POLISH: a corrupt saved `cond` on a truck (NaN, "abc", null) loads as 0% (65% power, $2000 repair) because `NUM()` returns 0, while the same corruption on the trailer loads as 100% (line 820 vs 813). Pick one; defaulting trucks to 100 like the trailer is the kinder choice.
3. Not tested: phone layout of the repair rows, trailer repair while hitched and towed to the garage, a used truck's repair price vs its Bytteboden ask.
