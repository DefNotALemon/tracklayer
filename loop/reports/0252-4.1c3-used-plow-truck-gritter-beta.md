## What held
- A used plow truck paid for and collected at its seller's lay-by arrives with an EMPTY hopper (0 kg), as the listing says.
- Fill refused at the lay-by (no cash taken, hopper stays 0). With the truck on the garage forecourt: 3 t for $150 ($50 a tonne); filling a full hopper costs $0.
- Spinner on and rolling 5 s of game time: hopper 3000 -> 2987 kg, the hint reads sensibly ("GRITTING · 3.0 t in the hopper · off the road: it is going on the snow").
- Save + reload: truck still owned, hopper 2987 -> 2986 kg (saved).
- Junk hopper values (NaN, 1e9, "x") through `TLXPL.tidy()`: no throw, clamped to 0..3000.

## Findings
1. [POLISH] The spinner is off after a reload (it was on before). Probably intended, but nothing tells the player that the gritter stopped. Either save the flag or leave it; a one-line toast on load would do.
2. [POLISH] `hopOf` (tlx-plow.js line 165) only floors at 0; a saved hopper above 3 t (hand-edited or from an older cap) reads as e.g. 1e9 kg until `tidy()` runs, and the hopper weighs that much to the physics in the meantime. Clamp at the read to `d.hop` as well.
3. [POLISH] Not tested: selling a used plow truck (no sell function exposed on `TLXV`, so the "blade/hopper record dropped with the truck" check was skipped); pad/touch hold-E to start the spinner on the used truck.
