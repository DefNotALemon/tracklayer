## Findings
1. [POLISH] On open ground in 60 cm of snow, drop-blade says "your wheels stand in snow it has not cut: back up to cleared ground and plow in from there". Correct and clear, but it fills the hint line so the "E: lift it · hold it: throw left" part wraps to a second line in the HUD. Fine on a 1280x720 screen; check it on a phone-size HUD.
2. [POLISH] The slew setting is shared across schemes and carries between taps (it is saved with the blade), so a tester has to read the hint ("hold it: X") to know the next throw; this is as designed. No field named `slew` on the runtime, so scenarios must read the hint.
3. [POLISH, scenario note] `sideCam` on a 3.5 m cab puts the camera inside the tanker body/cab (still shows only orange panels); use a hand-placed camera for stills of this truck. Not a game bug.
