# 0118: 2.2 vehicle swap across reloads, stripped and dangling saves: beta (main b241d38, v187)

Nothing new had landed since 0117 (only reports), so this tests 2.2 (vehicle interface, two reports so far). New scenario
(committed): `x2_swapreload_break.mjs`, 23 asserts, all pass, page errors none. No BLOCKER, no BUG found.

## What held
- Reload in the truck's seat: truck still current, you are still in the cab (not on foot, no phantom sled), same position (0.0 m), fuel 70 L kept.
- Reload mid get-off (`DMT.on`) and mid get-on (`MNT.on`): both load with no stuck animation, exactly one of on-foot / in-vehicle, no NaN. Both end seated in the truck (the animation is dropped, not resumed); from there Q out and Q back in work.
- Save with `x` stripped (old save, no trucks): loads on the sled, 0 trucks, no NaN.
- Corrupt `x.vehicles` with `cur: "v9"` that isn't owned: loads, `cur` cleared to null, you ride the sled, Q works, camera finite.
- Six Q taps in 0.7 s beside a second truck: settles in the cab after 5 s game time, no stuck mount/dismount, no NaN.

## Findings
1. POLISH: reload mid get-off puts you back in the driver's seat rather than on foot beside the truck (the save carries `cur` while FOOT is not saved). Harmless, but a player who saved by closing the tab while stepping out finds themselves seated again.
2. POLISH (harness limit, not a game bug): my "drive before reload" step held W 1.5 s facing the garage door at the forecourt, so the truck did not move (still image `x2sr_driving_reload`: speed 0, nose at the door). The reload-while-moving case (velocity, trailer, hitched sled) is therefore untested; 0.0 m drift only proves the parked case.
3. POLISH: the stripped-save and dangling-`cur` cases are silent, which is fine; a dangling `cur` does not log anything, so a refund/sale bug that left it would be invisible. Consider a console warn in the migrate.

Not tested: reload while moving or towing, reload with two trucks and a trailer hitched, truck at night, pad and touch Q during the reload window.
