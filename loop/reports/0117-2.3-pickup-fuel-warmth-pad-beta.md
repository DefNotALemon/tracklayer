# 0117: 2.3 pickup fuel, warmth, pad driving and standstill bogs: beta (main cc494b0, v187)

Nothing new had landed since 0116, so this tests the landed part with the fewest reports (2.3, two so far). New scenarios
(committed): `x2_pickupfuel_break.mjs` (tank, low-fuel warning, cab warmth, quay refill, dismount, pad drive) and
`x2_pickupspots_break.mjs` (seated truck, W held 5 s from rest at six flat open spots 0.6-2.6 km from the garage). Page errors
none. No BLOCKER. `x2_pickupfuel_break.mjs` still prints FAIL on its step 1 and the pad step when the teleport to the "open spot"
leaves the truck bogged; read finding 1, those are the same effect and not a second bug.

## What held
- Tank is 70 L as specced; the burn is finite and small; the low-fuel toast fires at 19% and names the nearest pump
  ("Fuel low. Nearest pumps: Kjøllefjord Drivstoff, 0.65 km, $2.45/L").
- Heated cab: warmth 60 -> 59.6 in 10 s away from shelter (the sled loses far more).
- Quay top-up works from the driver's seat (fuel 5 -> 54 L in 8 s game time).
- Q while the HUD says STUCK gets you out on foot (hint then reads "E: dig her out (slow) · Q: get on").
- Pad from the forecourt: RT + stick right drove 36 m in 5 s and yawed -1.52 rad; stick left turns it back. No NaN anywhere.
- Fuel is never above the tank cap after getting out.

## Findings
1. BUG (standing start in untouched snow): at 3 of 3 flat land spots (slope < 0.05, away from lanes) the pickup, from a
   standstill and W held, moved 0.0 m in 5 s and went to `BOG.on` ("Stuck to the axles. Q: get out, then E to dig, or N to
   reset."). The other three spots were frozen lakes (see 4). A player who parks off the plowed lane to deliver or to look
   around, then pulls away, hits this with no wheelspin phase to feel it coming: it goes straight from "fine" to STUCK. The
   toast does appear here (unlike 0116 #1), so it is not silent, but the truck never gets a chance to rock out. Suggest a
   short wheelspin-and-creep phase (a metre or two, tyres digging) before declaring a bog when the start is from rest, or let
   a few seconds of reverse/forward rocking clear it. Caveat: my arrival is a teleport (`P.y` dropped 0.3 m, speed 0), so the
   first frames may be harsher than a real arrival; still, 0108/0116 saw the same at honest stops.
2. BUG (bog persists across teleport/reset in my run): after a bogged truck is moved to the quay (plowed, flat, `GS.near ===
   depot`) `BOG.on` stayed true and `Q` offered the dig-out, pad RT then moved it 1.3 m in 5 s. Moving a bogged vehicle onto
   plowed ground should clear the bog (N reset does). Low severity: a player cannot teleport, but a tow/winch drag onto a lane
   would do the same; check `BOG.on` clears when the wheels touch a yard/lane.
3. POLISH: while bogged the truck still burns fuel at idle (0.3 L in 8 s) and keeps the heated cab; fine, just noting nothing
   draws attention to the engine labouring.
4. POLISH (ice): on three lake spots (ice 20-23 cm) the truck covered 6.0-6.2 m in 5 s from rest at v 2.3-2.5 m/s, about a
   third of its pace on the forecourt, with no hint that traction is poor; the HUD shows the "E: get off and fish" prompt
   from the driver's seat instead. Probably right physically; a "slippery" cue would help.
5. POLISH (harness): `GS.near` is truthy on the forecourt, so the garage's free fill and shelter warmth mask fuel and warmth
   numbers until the truck is driven away; the first run of the scenario showed 70.00 L after 8 s for that reason.

Not tested: a second truck's fuel record, refuelling at a village pump with the truck, touch driving of the truck (0.0116 #3 still open), towing with a low tank.
