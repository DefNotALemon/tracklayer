# 0315 — 2.2 fleet: boarding and leaving by pad (LB) and touch pills — main @ 1c6f84a (script v255), tester 1

Nothing new landed for milestones 1-4 or the base game since 0314 (no non-playtest commits since 09:00). 0314 left pad/touch boarding of a bought truck untested, so this covers it. New scenario `x2_fleet_pad_touch_beta.mjs`: buys a pickup, then LB off the sled, LB on, walks beside the parked truck, LB in and out, then touch (`INP.dev = "touch"`, touch-typed pointer events on `#tcOn` / `#tcQ`) on the truck. Headless, page errors none, all asserts pass on the final run.

## Findings
1. OK — pad: LB gets off the sled and on again; LB boards the parked pickup (`cur` = v1) and gets out. Foot hints read "LB: get on the sled" and "LB: get in the pickup". No NaN in P, camera or fuel.
2. OK — touch: riding shows the GET OFF pill, tapping it puts the rider on foot, GET ON shows and boards. The foot hint reads "tap GET ON" with no key or pad name.
3. POLISH — after buying a truck, `cur` stays null and you keep riding the sled. Boarding the truck means walking to it, which is as designed, but the garage gives no cue of where the truck is parked. Not a bug.
4. NOT TESTED — mid-mount reload, a `cur` truck dropped while a pill is held, or touch plus a stuck/bogged truck.

Testing notes (no game issue): a synthetic `pointerdown` without `pointerType: "touch"` flips `INP.dev` back to kbm, so the hint reverts to "Q:". Real touch events set the type. Mounting from 3 m takes over 10 s wall in headless, so waits must be 20 s.
