# 0364: 2.2 truck at a station pump: fill, pay, cash short, reload, get out (beta, main b590378)

New `x2_truckpump_swap_beta.mjs` (1 FAIL, page errors none) plus probes `x2_truckpump_dismount_probe.mjs`, `x2_truckpump_q_probe.mjs`, `_q_probe2.mjs`, `_q_probe3.mjs` (all committed). No contact sheet: the findings are numbers.

## What held
- Pickup (cap 70 L) parked at Kjøllefjord Drivstoff ($2.45/L) fills at about 6 L per game s; paid 88.00 for 35.9 L (expected 88.42, within the whole-dollar billing).
- Cash 3 at the pump: fill stops at 2.2 L, cash 0, never negative.
- Reload while filling: you come back seated in the truck, fuel 14.9 -> 28.0 (it kept filling at the pump), cash and cap sane, no NaN.
- Truck record keeps the filled fuel (69.99 in `owned[0].fuel`) after leaving; no billing while on foot.

## Findings
1. BUG: Q (get out) does nothing, silently, for a truck parked at one spot at the pump. Truck at the station point, yaw 0: 5 game s, `DMT.on` never starts, no toast, no hint (probe3 `d(0,0) yaw 0: BLOCKED`). Same point at yaw 1.57 or 3.14, or 8 m off in any direction, gets out in 1.6 s. Probes 1/2 (truck dropped on that point after a previous yaw) hit it 4 times, so it is a repeatable pocket, probably a pump/canopy collider that leaves the walker no clear exit. A player parked there is stuck in the cab with the pump running. Suggested fix: if no clear exit spot, try the other side / push the walker out along the clear direction, else say "No room to get out here, pull forward."
2. POLISH: the fill toast sums across a teleport: "Pulled off the pump: 115.0 L ... -$282" for a 70 L tank (probe2). Only reachable by moving P without leaving the pump (my harness), but a truck swap or tow from one pump to the same station could do the same: `fuelEnd` is not called when the tank is topped up and room reappears. Low priority.
3. POLISH: while on foot beside the filling truck, the pump keeps filling it to full (69.99) after you step out, billed correctly until full. Fine as design, but the "Tank full" toast does not fire for a truck you walked away from (no toast seen after the dismount).

Not tested: pad/touch Q at the pump (same Q path), the fuel cabins' truck fill, two trucks at one pump.
