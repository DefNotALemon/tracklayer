# 0110: 2.7 Bytteboden used vehicles, out-of-order purchases and reload: beta test (main 4033002, v185)

Focus: slice 2.7 (first beta report on it). Reran the slice's own `x2_vehmkt.mjs`: 30 asserts pass, page errors none. New scenario
`x2_vehmktbreak.mjs` (committed) tries to break it by buying around a paid-for vehicle. One BUG, one POLISH, no BLOCKER. No still (logic only).

## What held
- Slice scenario all green: pool gating (trailer for all, pickup from 6 deliveries), haggling, remote SEND vs in-person pay, lemon inspection (ask 2575 -> 2190),
  yard placement (7-8 m from seller, dry), 83% power at 50%, repair only within 40 m ($1000 for 50 points), reload keeps cond (61% trailer, drag x1.234), old trailer without `cond` rolls as built.
- Reload while a pickup is paid for but not collected: the `held` entry survives and stopping at the yard still hands over the keys.

## Findings
1. BUG: money can be locked in an order that can never be collected. `heldWhy`/`MKTV.blocked` only checks ownership at collect time, but the garage lets you
   buy the same thing while the order waits. Repro (scenario A): pay a used Fjordhenger trailer remotely ($ paid, state "wait"), then buy the new trailer in the
   garage Trucks tab ($950, accepted). Stop at the seller's yard for 10 game seconds: nothing is handed over, `held` stays 1, forever ("You already own a sled trailer."
   on the order page). You can't sell the owned trailer, so the paid-for one is dead money. Same for trucks (scenario C): pay a used pickup remotely, buy
   trucks at the garage up to 3 (`buy()` counts `owned` only, not held pickups): the held pickup then waits at "That's enough trucks (3)."
   Suggested fix: `buyHaul()` refuses (toast "A trailer you paid for is waiting at <seller>") when `GS.mkt.held` has a `veh` `haul`; `buy()` counts held pickups
   as `usedTrucks(held)` does; or refund the held order when collect becomes impossible.
2. POLISH: the garage Trucks tab still lists "$950" for the trailer and the truck buy rows while a vehicle order is pending, so nothing hints at the clash above.

Not tested: collect when the seller's yard has no clear spot (the "No room... pull forward" nag), a paid trailer collected while a sled is strapped on the owned one,
vehicles night/phone layout of the Vehicles tab.
