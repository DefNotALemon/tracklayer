# 0116: 2.1 truck reverse (fix 489b7d0, v187): beta break test (main c97664f)

Focus: the newest landed change, the reverse fix for 0108 #1 (tlx-vehicles.js v10). New scenario `x2_vehreverse_break.mjs`
(committed): pickup + trailer, driven off the lane and stopped, then pad LT full / 30% / 60%, touch BRAKE pedal, full lock
while reversing, S then W flip, night, 10% grade, reload. Builder's own `x2_vehreverse.mjs` rerun first: all 15 checks pass.
Page errors none. No BLOCKER. The scenario still prints FAIL on B, C and (at one spot) D, see findings; read its log, not just the exit code.

## What held
- Pad LT fully pulled at a standstill off the lane backs the truck up 6.7-12.5 m in 4 s.
- Night (`GS.hour` 23.5): S reverses 11.8-12.5 m, nothing odd.
- S then straight to W while rolling backwards: forward speed 6.4-7.1 m/s after 2 s, `rev` flag clears, no NaN.
- Full lock each way while reversing: all values finite, yaw turns the right way (-1.5 / +0.76 rad in the run where it moved).
- Reload with the truck owned and current: still owned, finite pose.
- 10% grade, backing uphill: it does not move but says "The wheels spin in reverse and dig in..." (no silent case there).

## Findings
1. BUG: reverse is still silent at some off-lane spots. At a flat spot (slope < 0.04, fresh depth < 0.3 m) S held 3 s moved
   the truck 0.1 m, with telemetry `rev 1, fx -6560, drag 1044, sink 0.05/0.04/0.04/0.04, BOG.acc 0, bog false`, no toast. The
   pull is six times the drag and the tyres sink nothing, so something else is holding it (candidate: the "the brake stops you"
   clamp `if (!rev && ... vfPrev >= 0 && vf < 0) vf = 0` or a ground-contact/`P.gnd` state after a teleport; try logging `vf`,
   `vfPrev`, `P.gnd` at that spot). Same input at another spot moved 12.5 m. Full lock A or D at that spot: 0.0-0.4 m in 4 s, yaw
   +-0.12 rad, also silent. The player sees 0108's symptom again: a truck that ignores S with no hint. Repro: run the scenario;
   D0/D1/D lines at the 5th `fresh()` spot (prints `D0 S only at the same spot: 0.1 m`).
2. BUG (pad): the trigger is nearly on/off for reverse. LT at 30%: `fx -1968` against drag 1.0-2.7 kN, moved 0.0-0.5 m in 5 s
   over three runs; 60%: 0.1-5.7 m (varies by spot, once 0.3 m). Only a full pull reliably backs up. Suggest a response
   curve that moves at any pull above ~20% (the standstill drag is a fixed ~1-2.7 kN, so subtract it from the pull rather than
   comparing it) and a dig/bog hint when LT is held and nothing moves, like the keyboard has.
3. POLISH/inconclusive (touch): with `SET.touch="on"` and a synthetic `pointerdown` (pointerType "touch") on `#tcBrk`, `TC.brk`
   latches to 1 on its own but in the full scenario `input.brk` read 0 and the truck stayed put (0.0-0.9 m, `rev 0`). I could not
   separate a harness artefact (earlier padSet left a fake pad, `INP.dev` flips) from a real touch-brake problem. Not verified;
   rerun with a real `Input.dispatchTouchEvent` on a fresh profile with no pad ever attached.
4. POLISH (scenario hazard): `fresh()` spots differ in how deep the powder is around the rear tyres (sink 0.46 m on two of
   them) so the same S hold gives 0 m or 12 m. Reverse is correct for the deep ones (dig warning/bog), but 3-5 s of silence
   before the toast reads like a dead truck; consider warning earlier when S is held in reverse and speed < 0.3 for 1.5 s.

Not tested: pad reverse with trailer on a ramp, reverse in water edge, truck reverse with a loaded trailer on 10%+ grade, two trucks.
