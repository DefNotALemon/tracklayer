## Findings
1. [POLISH] Gritter running, blade up, stopped at the shore: the hint says "E: drop the blade" and E drops the blade; the gritter stays on. There is no press that only turns the spinner off while stopped here (the blade's toggle never reaches the `B.grit` branch at lines 525-527 for a truck that already has a hopper). Not a regression (the fix correctly keeps fishing out), but the player who wants the grit off must get it from the tablet/other route. Fix: if `B.grit` and blade up, make the press toggle the gritter off first, or add "· gritter on" to the hint.
2. [POLISH] Reload with the blade down at the shore: truck comes back with the blade up (`down false`), the hint is the blade's "drop the blade · 101 cm of snow ahead". Fine and consistent, only noting blade-down is not saved, so a reload by a lake never leaves the player stuck with a blade down. (My setup forced 25 cm field then reloaded; depth on return was 101 cm, the natural drift.)
3. [info] Getting out (Q) beside a lake with the blade down: on foot the hint is "Q: get in the plow truck", E does nothing and no fishing starts while the truck is within reach. The blade stays down on the parked truck. Not wrong, but a player who steps off to fish at the truck must walk away from it first to get fishing's hint. Existing behaviour, unchanged by the fix.
