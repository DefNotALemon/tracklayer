# 0109: 2.5 universal hitch on pad and touch, overload, reload: beta test (main 9c6cd21, v184)

Focus: slice 2.5 (one earlier report, 0095), covering what 0095 listed as not tested. New scenario `x2_hitchpad.mjs`
(committed): 27 asserts, all pass, no BLOCKER, no BUG. Still: `~/.hermes/cache/scratch/tlloop/reports/0109.jpg` (touch HITCH button).

## What held
- Keyboard: free trailer near the pickup's ball, walker at the tongue: use = HITCH, hint "E: hitch the trailer to the pickup".
- Pads (Xbox, DualSense, Switch Pro): stick click hitches then unhitches, with the right label each time ("L3: hitch ...", "L3: unhitch ...",
  "Left stick click: hitch ..." on the Switch pad).
- Touch: the orange button reads HITCH, tap hitches and it flips to UNHITCH, tap again frees it; hint "tap HITCH · tap GET ON". No overlap with GET ON / stick.
- Overload: flatbed on the pickup with 1,600 kg of big load aboard is refused with "The pickup's receiver is rated for 1500 kg. That's 1725 kg." (125 kg flatbed counted). With the load gone it hitches.
- A hitched flatbed survives a reload: still on the pickup, coupler 0.000 m off the ball, moved 0.00 m.

## Findings
1. POLISH: on a pad that was just connected (no input yet) the on-foot hint still shows the keyboard text ("E: hitch ...") until the first pad
   input, and a Switch pad first showed "L3" before it was recognised (then "Left stick click"). Same as other prompts, harmless, just noting the first-frame lag.
2. POLISH (hardening, not reachable by play): a `GS.load` entry without `dest` throws in `updGameHud` (`j.dest.x`, tracklayer.js ~12962) every frame, and one
   without a name-like field throws a `replace` TypeError elsewhere. Real jobs always carry them; only worth a guard if a TLX system ever pushes its own load items.
3. POLISH: the trailer stood free ~1.2 m from the ball is hitched by the coupler "teleporting" onto the ball (known, listed under Left in EXPANSION 2.5); in the still it reads fine but is the one thing a pad/touch player sees every hitch.

Not tested: hitching to a bogged truck in powder, a second ball within reach (0095's `x2_hitchbreak` covers the sled+truck case), trailer on a slope/water.
