Builder's x10_layers covers shop, wading wet rate, damp drain, stove dry, save/old save. This pass attacks corrupt saves, buy edges, death, time skips, feedback and a phone.

## Findings (most important first)
1. [10.1][POLISH] Wetness has no visible cue beyond one "soaked with sweat" toast (no HUD/tablet readout, no sled/rider look). After the toast
   is gone the player can't tell whether they're still damp (wet 0.84 outdoors is invisible) or when it's dried, so "stop at a stove"
   is guesswork. Suggest a small damp/dry chip beside the warmth gauge, or a second toast when it drops under ~0.2 ("dry again").
2. [10.1][POLISH] Time skips don't dry you outdoors: wet 0.90 then GS.hour += 48 outdoors → 0.84 (drying runs only on tick dt, not on skipped
   hours; cotton dries 1/900 s anyway). A long sleep away from a stove (tent in 10.3?) or a summer skip will leave you "soaked" on waking.
   Suggested: when hours are skipped, apply `wet -= b.dry * skippedSeconds` (or clear it) unless in the water.
3. [10.1][POLISH] `TLXG.buy("hat","x")` throws (`LAYERS[slot].find is not a function` for a junk slot such as "__proto__"/"hat"
   resolves to a non-array). Unreachable from the UI (buttons come from LAYERS), only scripted/console callers. Guard with `Array.isArray`.
4. [10.1][POLISH] Phone 390x844: the shop panel fits (no sideways scroll, buy buttons 302x37 px), but the seven tabs wrap to two rows with
   "Mid layers" alone on the second row and the buy cards need scrolling to see the Down jacket's button. Usable; only cosmetic.

- Corrupt saved state, `x.sys.gear` = wet "abc" → wet 0; wet 5 → clamps (0.87 after a second of drying); unknown base/mid ids and `own: null` → cotton/none, no NaN in
  ST.warm or coldK; null / string / number / array states all boot and fall back to defaults (separate boots; ~6 reloads in one run
  exhausts WebGL contexts in headless, a harness limit not a game bug).

- Blackout while soaked (wet 0.9, down jacket) doesn't throw; you wake at the quay and the stove dries you.
