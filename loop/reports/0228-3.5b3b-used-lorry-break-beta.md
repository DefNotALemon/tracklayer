## Findings (most important first)
1. [BUG] Truck cap is bypassed by an in-person lorry purchase while a used pickup is paid for and waiting. Setup: 2 trucks owned, a used pickup paid by SEND (held), a tractor listing that was already on the board. Paying the lorry at the seller's door hands it over (3 owned + 1 held = 4 committed). The held pickup is then silently dropped/refunded by `heldRefund` when `blocked()` sees `owned.length >= MAX_OWNED` (end state in the run: 3 trucks, 0 held). Money comes back, but the player loses the pickup they bought without choosing to. The pool and garage already count held orders (report 0110); `mktPay` for a listing already on the board does not. Suggested fix: in the pay path call `usedTrucks(heldList()) >= MAX_OWNED` (same toast as line ~1095) before taking cash for a pickup/tractor listing.
2. [POLISH] Listing board can't hold a tractor and a pickup listing together once one is paid for, which is fine, but a listing bought-then-blocked gives no toast on the pickup side beyond the refund line; consider naming the lorry as the reason ("no room for a 4th truck").
