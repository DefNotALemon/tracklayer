Tried to break slice 2.4's Map vehicle pins (`tabVehPts` + the "vehicles" layer) with two Fjellvogn pickups owned: in each cab, on foot beside each machine, sled <-> truck swaps, truck 1 driven 190 m away and left, reload in the cab, M and pad d-pad up in the cab.

## Findings
1. [POLISH] Two owned trucks both read "Pickup" on the Map. Bought back to back they park 8 m apart, so the two "Pickup" labels (and pins) stack on top of each other and on the quay/depot labels (x2cm_cab_map, x2cm_foot_map; pins at -1258,-386 and -1262,-379). Fix: label by uid ("Pickup 1/2") or plate/colour, nudge labels apart when pins are closer than ~20 px.
2. [POLISH] The vehicle pins have no layer chip. Chips are Deliveries / Pickup & drop-off / Rescues / Depots / Fuel; S/T pins and the "Sled" / "Pickup" labels can't be hidden and add clutter near the quay on the whole-island view. Fix: a "Vehicles" chip (default on) or hide labels at the whole-map zoom.
3. [POLISH] Pad d-pad up in the cab opened the tablet on the Map app rather than Home (the app last used, not a bug if intended; just noting it differs from Tab which showed Home in report 0093).

## Verified (no bug found)
