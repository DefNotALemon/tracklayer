# 0317 — 4.2b1 drive marks: saved-mask length cap vs real drive lengths (G4) — main @ 4311fb3
Scenario: tools/playtest/scenarios/x4_marklen_beta.mjs (new)   Shots: none (save/state test)

Part tested: drive part-cleared marks (`mk`/`ms`, beta fix 0250 #1). `drvTidy` accepts a drive mask only if it matches `/^[01]{1,64}$/`, one char per metre; lots use `{1,2000}`. Nothing had a beta report of its own (x4_drivemarks.mjs only).

## Findings
1. [POLISH] No bug today. All 8 drives are 10 m (DRV.len [6,10]), so the 64-char cap is never reached. Planted a half-cut mask at each kind's full length (drive 10, lot 274), reloaded through a real save: both kept their marks, page errors none. The cap would silently drop progress if drives ever got longer than 64 m (e.g. street or road-side drives from 4.x/5.x). Suggested fix: derive the cap from `DRV.len`/`c.L` rather than a literal, or raise it to 2000 like lots.
2. [POLISH] x4_drivemarks.mjs asserts the junk-mask cases in memory only; the new scenario adds the length-at-limit case through a reload.

- Existing drive/lot marks survive a reload at their real lengths.
