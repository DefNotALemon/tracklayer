Nothing new landed since 0133, so I took slice 2.2 (the nearest-vehicle swap and "get in the pickup"), which has no report on input schemes. Setup: truck bought at the garage, walker 5.5 m from the sled and 6.5 m from the pickup, then standing 3 m from each.

## Findings
1. [POLISH] Touch never says "pickup". Keyboard reads `Q: get in the pickup`, Xbox `LB: get in the pickup`, DualSense `L1: …`, Switch `L: …`, but touch reads `tap GET ON` with a button labelled `GET ON` (still of the pickup: x2gi_touch). On a phone you can't tell whether the button boards the sled or the truck when both are close, and you don't get on a truck. Suggest `tap GET IN` / button `GET IN` when `VEH.board` is set (the `BTN.mount` touch label is the static "GET ON").
2. [POLISH] Reloading while on foot beside the pickup puts you back in the driver's seat (foot true before, false after, no mount animation), and a reload mid-mount does the same. No loss of state, no NaN, truck still owned. Probably the same for the sled; worth knowing because Q after the reload gets you OUT, not in.

## Verified (no bug found)
- Standing between sled and truck, the hint follows the nearer one with no flicker: sled side reads `Sled 6 m on your right · walk back and press Q`, truck side `Q: get in the pickup`.
- Pads (Xbox / DualSense / Switch Pro): hint uses LB / L1 / L, never `Q:`. LB gets in the pickup and gets out again (the mount takes under 4.5 s of game time).
- Touch: `tcFoot` shows GET ON, the on-foot hint has no key names; no key names in either touch string.
- Reload mid-mount and reload on foot: finite positions, truck still owned, no page errors.
