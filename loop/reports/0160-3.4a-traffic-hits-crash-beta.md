2. [Verified] Six head-on hits in a row on the same car at 15 m/s: each bounces out at about 6 m/s, no tunnelling, no accumulating error, position finite.
3. [POLISH] The "Bump / Crunch" toast and the `hits` count have a 6 s per-car cooldown (`c.bumpT`). Back-to-back hits at 3.5 s spacing alternate toast / no toast (hits 1,0,1,0,1,0). Intended by the code, but a 45 m/s head-on straight after a 20 m/s one gets only the thud and shake, no "Crunch". Suggest a harder hit (> 9 m/s) bypassing the cooldown.
4. [POLISH] A 45 m/s (160 km/h) head-on into a car does not eject the rider. Boulders do (crashEject at speed); a solid car at that speed arguably should. Not a fault, a consistency question.
5. [Verified] Crash ragdoll flung toward a car from 30 m: the car stops for it, ragdoll's closest approach 6.2 m, car v 0.6 afterwards, no error, positions finite. After being left alone for 9 game s a hit car pulls away again.
