## Findings (most important first)
1. [BUG] The pack's 120-round cap is broken by rounds returning from the magazine. `fit()` (tlx-guns.js ~line 157) does `pkAdd(g.ammo, g.rounds - cap)` with no cap check: a full 10-round magazine (det10) plus a full pack (117), then fitting the 3-round magazine, gave a pack of 127 (match308 60 -> 67), and further load swaps carried 130. `loadAmmo` can do the same (magazine rounds back into a full pack). Nothing is lost in play, but the shop's "pack stops at 120" rule no longer holds, and on reload `load()` clamps to 120, so the surplus (7-10 rounds) silently vanishes. Suggested fix: when returning rounds, keep what fits under `AMMO.max` and leave the rest in the magazine (or refuse the swap with a toast "pack is full").
2. [POLISH] Not tested live: pad/touch ammo wheel is covered by x11_ammo (passes); the pack-over-cap case above was reached by direct state setup plus the real `fit`/`loadAmmo` calls, so a player needs a full pack and a full big magazine to hit it (rare).
