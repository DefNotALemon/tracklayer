The truck stops for nothing, no toast, no bump, no damage. Compare the sled's own tow rig (`towStep`), which has snag probes and says "Trailer slammed into rock". Same path will hold for trees, houses and parked cars; only rocks were tried. Suggested fix: reuse the probe loop from `towStep` (or a 2-3 circle chain along the deck) in `haulFollow`, pushing the trailer out and, past ~3 m/s, a `bump()`-style hit and a yank on the truck while reversing.
