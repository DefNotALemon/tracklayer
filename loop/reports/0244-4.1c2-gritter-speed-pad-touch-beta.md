## What held
- DualSense L3 held while the truck rolls at ~9 m/s starts the gritter with the blade up. The hint says "hold L3: gritter off" and the hopper drops 2996 -> 2975 kg over 4 s. A second hold stops it.
- Touch: the BLADE DOWN pill held while rolling starts it (hint "hold BLADE DOWN: gritter off"). A tap then drops the blade and the gritter runs on, pill reads BLADE UP.
- A 25 kg hopper driven 9 s empties, the spinner stops, toast "The hopper is empty: the spinner has stopped. The garage's Plow tab fills it.", and the hopper stays at 0 (never negative).
- Storm at hour 23.5: the gritter runs, stretch share counts (10% after 6 s), no NaN.

## Findings
1. [POLISH] `pointercancel` on the pill mid-hold (a finger dragged off the screen or a system gesture) does nothing: no slew, no gritter toggle, no stuck state. That is the right result. It is logged so nobody "fixes" it.
2. [POLISH] After a touch run the hint read "off the road: it is going on the snow" while the blade was down and the truck had wandered from the line of my scripted driver. It is correct, but the line is long on touch: blade hint plus gritter hint is about 190 characters. Check that it fits the phone HUD (not measured here).
3. [POLISH] Pad: the hold was started with the stick centred, so a stick-driven (R2 + stick) run was not covered. Speed rose 9 -> 11 m/s during the hold because my keyboard W was still held. That is an artefact, not a bug.

No BLOCKER or BUG found.
