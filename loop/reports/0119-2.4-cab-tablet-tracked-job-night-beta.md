## Findings
1. POLISH (third report of it: 0093, 0099, now): Q out of the truck with the tablet open leaves the overlay open on the walker (open true, mode overlay, app jobs). Close it when `dismount()` starts.
2. POLISH (existing behaviour, not a 2.4 regression, but harsh in a truck): claimed parcels, cargo in the bed and the tracked job are not saved (`save()` has no `load`/`claims`; the code comment says so). Closing the tab or reloading with 2 parcels loaded drops them silently (claims 1 -> 0, jobs 0 after reload in `x2_cabjob_ctrl`). If bonds were paid on them they are gone too. Consider saving `GS.load`/`GS.claims`, or a one-line note on the Parcels app that cargo doesn't survive a reload.
3. BUG? (unconfirmed cause, same family as 0099 #5): the truck from rest on flat open snow does not move on W. At three obstacle-free spots (slope 0.008-0.017, nearest object over 40 m) with four headings each, speed stays 0.0 for 4 s of W; 7 of 12 runs show `BOG.on` true within that time, the rest sit still unbogged, even where snow depth is 0.06 m. S (reverse) from the same kind of spot moves it -8 m, and W on a plowed lane moves 11.9 m at 9 m/s. Caveat: I teleport `P` between runs, so the bog state may be a teleport artefact; a player driving off the lane onto powder is the real check. Suggest: drive the truck off a lane edge by hand and log depth, speed and bog when it stops.
4. POLISH: a Bytteboden toast ("600 liquid twin") sits over the top of the Jobs app, covering the "2 jobs - compass" subtitle line (still, right panel). Same family as 0099 #2.
5. POLISH (harness note): `x2_cabjob_break.mjs`'s reverse step fails at `depot+90` (truck at -1210,-295 does not move on S either, tablet or not). That is finding 3's spot, not a tablet bug; `x2_cabjob_ctrl2.mjs` does the reverse check on a lane and passes.

Not tested: cab with a trailer hitched while the tablet is open, pad/touch J cycling in the cab, tracked job completing while the tablet is open.
