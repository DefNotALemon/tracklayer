# 0154 — 3.2b2 scraped road, natural cycle (v203) beta — main @ d2c7659
Scenario: new `x3_scraped_cycle_break.mjs` (month sweep, weather, heading sweep, sleep skip, live plow, day run) + `x3_scraped_clock_probe.mjs`. Result: all asserts pass, page errors none. Stills `x3sc_day_side` / `x3sc_day_rear` (sheet in scratch `reports/0154.jpg`). Not tested: pad/touch (no prompts in this slice), trailer rig on a scraped track.

## Findings
1. [Verified] Season window: clear noon with a fresh plow scrapes Nov (-4 C) 0.16, Dec/Jan/Feb/Mar 1.00 (-8 to -11 C), Apr (-3 C) 0, May (+2 C) 0. All values finite and within 0..1. Note March is as fully scraped as January (full at -7 C); intended per the header comment, just worth knowing.
2. [Verified] Sleep skip: an old-plow stretch given 0.6 snow, then `GS.hour += 6` → snow 0, scrapeF 1.00 (stamped "plowed 1 h ago" on wake, by design). A skip over an already clear old road stays 0, so a night's sleep does not scrape roads that never snowed. Live plow: snow 0.5 → cleared within 2 half-hour steps and scraped 1.00 straight away.
3. [Verified] Heading: down the track and back the other way read the same (0.50 each); no NaN at 15/30/60/90/180/270 degrees. Weather front (WX.at 1.0) only moves the temperature from -11 to -8 C, still fully scraped; a real storm kills it through road snow instead, as designed.
4. [POLISH] A sled lying 90°/270° across a wheel track reads P.scr 1.00 (double the 0.50 of driving along it), because the skis' ±0.55 m offset then runs along the road and all three samples land in the same rut. Only matters in a spin-out on a freshly plowed road (extra drag and sparks for a moment). If wanted, cap by the sled's angle to the road.
5. [POLISH] Sparks still hard to read by day. `x3sc_day_side` (11:04 twilight, 54 mph in the track, P.scr 0.5, 145 sparks counted, HUD "Scraped road"): the sparks show as small white dots indistinguishable from the snow flecks, no orange streak at the skis (same as 0152 #3 at night). Fix: warm emissive colour and a little longer life/size for scrape sparks, or a faint warm point light at the skis.
6. [Note, harness] Setting `GS.hour` past mid-June (day ~200+) makes the calendar wrap to "1 Nov" and shifts the calendar offset: afterwards `GS.hour = <January>` reads as 24 May (+2 C) and nothing scrapes. Scenarios must not jump the clock into summer and back; not a slice bug (it is the summer-skip wrap), but it silently makes later weather-dependent asserts read 0.
