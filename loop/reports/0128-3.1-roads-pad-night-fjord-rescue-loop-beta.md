Scenarios: new `x3_roadpad_night_break.mjs` (pickup on the Gamvik/Dyfjord roads at 23:00 with a scripted Xbox pad, mid-drive reload, touch UI), probes `x3_roadsea_probe.mjs`, `x3_roadsea_rescue_probe.mjs`, `x3_roadpad_dyfjord_probe.mjs`. Page errors none. Still: `~/.hermes/cache/scratch/tlloop/reports/0128.jpg` (pickup stuck in the fjord, "IN THE WATER · F: CALL FOR HELP").

## Findings
1. BLOCKER (softlock + money drain): a pickup that goes into the fjord cannot be rescued. Test spot (1493, -2822), 10 m off the Gamvik road. P puts it in the water (`P.wet`, sink 0.58, drag ~9450, v 0, `HELP.on`). The rescue works as designed for 33 game s (approach, hook, pull, -$140, toast "Winched out of the fjord"), puts the truck on land (y 1.9, ground 2.0 at 1486, -2829) and 3 s later the truck is back in the water 3.6 m away (1488, -2826, ground -0.5): `startDrown` fires again, "Through the ice and into the fjord!", HELP phase "wait". Nothing was held. Idle for 36 more s it stays wet. Each cycle costs $140 and the player is back where they started. `helpDone` lands it only `+4 m` past the shore point with `BOG.immune = 6`; the pickup is long (wheelbase 3 m, tail overhang) and the bank is a slope, so it rolls or its rear probes into water. Suggest: in `helpDone`, for a vehicle (`VEH.cur`) put it at least 12-15 m inland on ground whose surrounding 8 m ring is not water, zero velocity, and hold it (no wet check) for ~3 s. Check `x3_roadsea_rescue_probe.mjs` lines `r 33` and `r 36`: expect `wet:false` still at `r 60`. Not tested: the same spot with a sled (the code is shared; the sled may roll less).
2. BUG: the water hint names a key that does nothing. "IN THE WATER · F: CALL FOR HELP" (`tracklayer.js` ~15359) and the dunk toast "Press F to call for help" (~14793) are hard-coded text. The binding is P (`gameKey KeyP`, line 8547). Tapping F: `HELP.called` stays false (`GB.dir` unchanged). A player in the water reads F, presses it, nothing happens. P works (called, eta 34 s, fee 140).
3. BUG: pad has no way to call for help. I held each pad button 0-15 (except Menu) for 250 ms while in the water: none set `HELP.called`. The hint under a pad (`INP.dev` "pad") still reads "F:". Touch has its own button (`tcF: CALL HELP`, shown only in the water or at an empty tank), so only the pad is stuck. Together with 2 this breaks Lemon's standing rule (control text from `btn(action)`, pads show matching names). Suggest a `help` action in `BTN` with P / a free pad button (not B, A or Y; D-pad down?) / touch tcF, and use `btn("help")` in both strings and `keyHint`.
4. BUG (minor, harness-found): `P.wet` and `P.sink` survive a teleport. After the sea, `place()` onto the Dyfjord road left `wet:true, sink 0.58, drag 9454` on dry land: the truck sat at v 0 with RT held for 20 s, `stuckT` 4+, no toast, no STUCK prompt (`#flip` stays hidden). A real player gets there only through N (reset) / a mid-sea reload; the reload case was fine (reload resets). Check N while wet: after N in the sea the truck stayed at the same place with `wet:true` too (N did nothing, no toast). Suggest: N while wet = say "call for help instead" or reset out of the water.
5. VERIFIED: pad on the roads. With the pad (RT + left stick), pickup speeds on the Dyfjord and Gamvik lines match the keyboard (19.4 vs 19.4 m/s at 6 s on dyfjord idx 30%, 21.7 vs 21.7 on gamvik), no bog, no page errors. `INP.dev` switches to pad and back to kbm on a key. Mid-drive reload at night: same vehicle, same position (1695.8, -3039.6), roads rebuilt (`TLXR.ready`, no err, 860 poles). Touch UI after the reload shows STEER, GAS, BRAKE, HOP, LEAN, WHEELIE, HORN, GET OFF. GET IN with a pad is the LB button ("LB: get in the pickup"), hint correct.
6. POLISH: the Gamvik road runs 3 m from the fjord at idx ~159 and 21 of its points are within 20 m of water, with no bank or marker (Dyfjord min 9 m, Ifjordfjellet 30 m; the other three routes none). My pad pursuit drove into the sea at off-line 13 m because the road is 10 m from the shore. With a pickup this means a $140 mistake (and finding 1). A snow bank / pole line on the sea side, or keeping the route at least 12 m back, would stop the casual drive-in.
