# 0316 — 5.4a1 plow driver (own truck): hostile SAVED `pdr` record through a real reload (G4, lane A) — main @ 94d52eb (script v255, tlx-plow v22), tester 1
Scenario: tools/playtest/scenarios/x5_plowdriver_pdr_hostile_beta.mjs (11 cases, each planted with `save()` then a real `reload()`, then 3 h of clock with snow on two stretches). No stills (state/number test).

## Findings (most important first)
1. **[BUG] A saved driver position `p` far past the road is never tidied, and a driver "out" or "back" with it never works again.** `pdrTidy` only checks that `p` is a finite number >= 0, not that it is <= the road's length. Planted `{st:"out", p:1e12}` (and `{st:"back", p:9e9}`): after reload the driver stays `st:"back"`. Over 3 h of clock `p` fell by only 3.7e4 m (12 km/h), the two snowed stretches stayed at 0.5 / 0.6 m, no pay, no notification, no warning cleared: the driver is "driving back" for about 80,000 years of game time. The Plow/Staff cards read clean, so the player sees a driver who never turns up, while the kommune's late rule still strikes the stretches. Only handing the road back or firing the driver clears it. A save with `p` over the road length is unlikely from real play (`p` is capped at `far`/`r.len` while out), but a road that is shortened or re-routed by an update would give exactly this. Fix: in `pdrTidy`, `q.p = Math.min(q.p, r.len)` (needs the route; or clamp in `pdrRun` before the `back` branch: `q.p = Math.min(q.p, r.len)`).
2. **OK** `p` negative, `p` a string ("abc"), `st` an object, huge `w`, huge/negative `n`, `cash`, `wkN`, `wkCash`, a wrong `who`, a `truck` id on an own-truck driver, `hold`/`rc` as strings: all tidied on load (finite, non-negative, valid `st`), no exception, the driver sets out and opens both stretches within 3 h, pay +$120 (2 stretches at 40% kept, exactly as an unplanted run), home afterwards. Huge `n`/`cash`/`wkCash` do not leak into pay or notifications.
3. **OK** Cards (Staff, Plow) and notifications in every case contain no `undefined` / `NaN` / `null` / `Infinity` / `[object`.
4. **POLISH (test note)** The "p within the road" check also fails for the case after a huge-p one (`w huge`: p 9.0e9 carried over from the previous case's unhealed record); it self-heals the moment the driver reaches `home` (p reset to 0). This confirms finding 1: only the `home` state clears a bad `p`.
