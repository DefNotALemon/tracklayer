# 0246 — 4.2a Plow app / road contracts, break test — main @ fc9e0c2 (tlx-plow v11, tlx-roads v14)
Scenarios: `x4_plowapp.mjs` re-run (all ok, page errors none). New `x4_plowapp_break.mjs` (exit 1: 2 FAIL, page errors none). Headless, day-time stepped clocks, plow truck, Dyfjord road, keyboard-less (contract API). Not tested: pad focus on the app, touch, night, old save.

## Findings
No BLOCKER.
1. BUG — HAND BACK + SIGN launders warnings. Signing creates a fresh record (`strikes: 0`). A road with 2 warnings handed back and signed again reads strikes 0 (scenario N), so the "three warnings and the road is taken back for a week" rule can be dodged forever at no cost. Suggest: keep strikes (and the weekly clean flag) in a per-road record that survives HAND BACK, or ban re-signing for a while after a hand-back at 1+ strike.
2. BUG — HAND BACK dodges a shut stretch's clock. Stretch 2 shut 7 h (`due[2] = [7.0, 1]`), handed back and signed again: `due[2]` is `[3.0, 1]` after 3 more hours (10 h in all, no warning). The clock restarts on re-sign and the 8 h late warning never lands (scenario M). 0245's author already noted "free hand back dodges a warning"; this is the proof it can be chained. Same fix as 1: warn/strike on hand-back while a stretch is shut past the late line, or keep the clock.
3. POLISH — two roads can be signed at once (2 held); fine per the design, just noting no cap exists. The shortest-blade case (unfit while signed) could not be tested: a plow truck's own blade is factory and refuses to come off, so a contract can only lose its blade by selling the truck — worth a pass.

Suggested priority: 1 and 2 together (same cause: sign/quit hold no per-road memory).
