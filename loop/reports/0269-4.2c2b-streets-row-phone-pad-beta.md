At 667x375 the text span is 42 px wide and 263 px tall; at the itch embed size 640x360 it is 15 px wide and 737 px tall, the row 751 px (tablet pane 265x170). Still shows "kommu / ne's" one word piece per line. Portrait 390x844 is fine (120 px wide, row 102 px). Fix: let the row wrap as two lines under ~480 px wide (name + pill above, text below), or `grid-template-columns:1fr auto` with the text spanning a second row. The same markup is used for the lot-window rows (`plw:`) in the same app, which probably squeeze alike (not measured).
2. BUG (same cause) — at 640x360 the row sits 146 px above the pane top and is 751 px tall, so even scrolled to its middle you read a thin ribbon. Unreadable on the published itch embed size.
3. OK — touch tap on the row: app stays open, no text change, no Jobs/waypoint side effect. POLISH: the row has `data-tf` so the d-pad focuses it, but A/tap does nothing; the Jobs row is where the heap is tracked. Tapping it to track or pin the heap would match the lot rows.
