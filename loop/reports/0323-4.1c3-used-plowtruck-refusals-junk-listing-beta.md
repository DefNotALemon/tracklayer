# 0323 — 4.1c3 used plow truck: refusals and corrupt listing fields — main @ 4ceee3b (v256, tlx-vehicles v32)
Scenario: NEW `tools/playtest/scenarios/x4_plowused_refuse_beta.mjs` (fresh profile, listings made with `mktMake`, bought with `mktPay`, read through `mktPayWhy`). Earlier 4.1c3 reports covered collection, mend, reload and the hopper; none tried the refusals or a bad listing.

## Findings
1. OK — no Freight licence: `mktPayWhy` says "A plow truck needs your Freight licence."; `mktPay` moves $0 and holds nothing. (The generator also makes no plow-truck listing at all while you lack the licence.)
2. OK — three trucks owned: "That is enough trucks on the forecourt (3)."; no cash moves.
3. OK — two plow-truck listings paid back to back: only one is held, second one's cash not taken (cash 90,000 -> 76,945).
4. POLISH — a listing with `ask = NaN` is bought with no refusal (`payWhy` ""): cash becomes NaN and one truck is held. A listing with `ask = -100` is bought and PAYS the player $100. `mktPay` / `payWhy` don't check `Number.isFinite(ask) && ask > 0`. Needs a corrupt save (line 10722 of tracklayer.js only filters on `cat`), so unlikely in play, but a NaN cash is unrecoverable. Suggested fix: in the load filter at tracklayer.js:10722 also drop listings whose `ask` isn't a finite positive number, or refuse in `mktPay`.
5. OK — `adv`/`real` undefined, `life` NaN, `site` "nowhere": no throw, cash finite, charged normally (the listing is just paid at its ask). `ask` 1e12: refused, no cash moves. Market tablet opens, page errors none.
