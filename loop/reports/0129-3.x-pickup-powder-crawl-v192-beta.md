## Findings
1. [BUG, med] Feathered pad throttle stalls dead in 35 cm with no feedback. Flat spot, pad RT 30%: 0.15 m -> 7.6 m (vmax 2.3); 0.35 m -> 0.0 m, vmax 0, BOG false and `#wnHud` hidden, so the truck just sits and the player gets no STUCK/DIGGING hint for 7 s. (Pad RT 60% crawls 12.9 m per x3_crawl, so the cliff sits between 30 and 60%.) Suggest: show the "WHEELS DIGGING IN / more throttle" hint whenever throttle > 0.1 and speed < 0.3 m/s in deep snow, and/or raise the pull-away floor at low throttle.
2. [BUG, low-med] The 35 cm crawl is very slope sensitive. W from rest on 35 cm: 1.9% slope 4.5 m, bogs at 5.6 s (vmax 1.3); the near-zero-slope spot in x3_crawl gives 13.3 m with no bog. A 2% grade cuts the crawl by two thirds. On the 10.7% uphill, 20 cm powder (flat: fine, ~30 m at 25 cm) moves 0.1 m ("WHEELS DIGGING IN"), 30 cm and 35 cm bog in 3.8-4.4 s; bare snow on the same hill climbs 29 m at 13 m/s. Probably correct for a truck, but the builder's "uphill untested" note: a 20 cm drift on a ~10% road is now a hard wall. Consider whether that matches the intent ("slows to walking pace by 35 cm").
3. [POLISH] Reverse is more forgiving than forward: S in 35 cm moves 7.9 m (vmax 1.8) without bogging versus W 4.5 m + bog at the same spot. Fine as an escape, but a bogged truck (40 cm, W 6 s) then S held 5 s moves only 0.09 m; escape is on foot only, as before.
4. [POLISH] Stop/start inside the crawl works: W 3 s, release 1.5 s, re-press: total 5.2 m, no bog, hint "WHEELS DIGGING IN" shown. Reads fine.
5. [Verified] Night (GS.hour 23) gives identical numbers to day (35 cm flat 4.5 m bog 5.6 s; hill 30 cm bog 4.4 s), no NaN anywhere, camera finite.
