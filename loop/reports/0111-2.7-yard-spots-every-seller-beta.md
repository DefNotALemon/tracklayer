# 0111: 2.7 used vehicles, collect spot at every seller's yard: beta test (main ea0a96d, v185)

Focus: slice 2.7, second beta report. 0110 left "collect when the yard has no clear spot" untested. New scenario `x2_vehmktyards.mjs` (committed) calls the real
`mktCollect(site)` for a paid used pickup and a used trailer at all 18 sites that list vehicles (skjanes, dyfjord, neset, solbakken, lebesby, sandfjord,
myrvang, brattli, naust, utsikten, mehamn, gamvik, windfarm, ifjord, strandbu, fjellstua, risfjord, relay), each three ways: rider on the sled 12 m away, rider
on foot 3 m further, and the rider parked exactly on the first-choice spot. 108 collects. Exit 0, page errors none. No BLOCKER, BUG or POLISH new. No still (logic only).

## What held
- 108 of 108 collects found a spot: dry, off the sea, no obstacle within 1 m, 8.0 m from the seller (pickup) / 7.0 m (trailer; its position is the body centre, so 7.0 is the 8 m spot), never within 6.9 m of the rider.
- Rider parked on the first-choice spot: the vehicle goes to the next candidate (about 7.8 m from the rider), no stacking. On foot: same.
- `held` empties on success every time; nothing collected twice.
- So the "No room ... Pull forward a bit" nag never fired at any real seller. The nag path itself (all candidates blocked) is unreached by real sites; not testable without faking obstacles.

## Findings
1. Still open from 0110 (not re-fixed on main, no code has landed since): BUG, pay a used trailer/pickup remotely, buy the same kind at the garage meanwhile, and the paid order can never be collected (dead money). Suggested fix as in 0110.
2. NOTE (not a bug): the scenario's first draft failed every trailer row because the trailer record stores the body centre (1 m nearer than the spot); anything asserting "8 m from the seller" on `s.haul` must allow that.

Not tested: night and phone layout of the Vehicles tab, the nag path with a truly full yard, collect while a sled is strapped on an owned trailer.
