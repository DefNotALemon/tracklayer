## Findings
1. POLISH: on the Map app at full-tablet zoom the roads are barely readable. They are thin orange lines in nearly the same orange-brown as the contour lines (still, top right); only the minimap shows them clearly. Make the road line wider/darker or a distinct colour on the big map, and give a legend entry or a Roads filter chip next to Deliveries/Fuel.
2. POLISH: walking on the plowed road is not faster than walking in the powder 40 m beside it (5.9 vs 5.8 m / 4 game s on this stretch; 4.8 average with a slow first run). Plowed ground gives a rider on foot no benefit, so a road feels like nothing when you are off the sled. Check footStep reads `yardAt` pack, or accept it as intended.
3. POLISH (harness note): the first on-foot walk after a dismount runs ~35% short (3.9 vs 5.9 m) from the standing start/gait warm-up; scenarios must discard it.
4. POLISH: at night on the road, with the sled's lights on, the road is not distinguishable from the surrounding snow in the still, and no pole was within the beam; poles are only a help if they sit in the headlight cone. Worth a night drive along 200 m of road to judge.

No BLOCKER or BUG found. Not tested: pad/touch Map road legibility, the closed Ifjordfjellet road on foot, driving a full route end to end.
