## Findings (most important first)
1. [14.1][BUG] A full storm (GS.storm = 1, any hour) sinks the skiff at speed. At 8.3 m/s the swell heave dips the hull to y -0.41 m, which trips the game's drown test (`P.y < wl - 0.4`, tracklayer.js ~13443). `startDrown()` runs: "IN THE WATER · P: CALL FOR HELP", boat frozen at 0 m/s, $140 rescue. Heave range by storm strength, 8 s at full throttle: 0.3 gives -0.19..0.21, 0.6 gives -0.30..0.26, 1.0 gives -0.41..0.21 and drowns. A boat in heavy weather should slam and slow, not drown its skipper. Fix: clamp the heave floor to about -0.3, or make the drown test skip a boat afloat.
2. [14.1][POLISH] Running out of fuel at speed leaves the skiff drifting ("Out of fuel" shows, 2.7 m/s after 3 s of W, then stops). Seated idle on the water for 7 s and 6 s dead in the water did NOT drown the skipper, so the 4 s still-water rule is handled. No rescue offer for a dry tank on water was checked (only the toast).
