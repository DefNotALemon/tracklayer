# 0245 — 4.1b2b plow blade trip, kinds / offsets / lift while folded — main @ dfd858d (v250, tlx-plow v10)
Scenario: new `tools/playtest/scenarios/x4_bladetrip_kinds.mjs` (exit 0, page errors none). Headless, day, keyboard W/E, pickup with a b23 blade, cleared 6 cm strip, edge reset to 100 per run. Not tested: pad, touch, night, a plow truck's own blade (only the pickup's), old save.

## Findings
No BLOCKER or BUG. Verified:
- Rock, log and parked car each trip the blade at 7 m/s (edge 100 -> 94.1 each, toast names the thing).
- Slewed blade (throw right and left) still trips (fold 0.60 rad).
- Glancing offsets, rock r 0.6, 14 m ahead, 8.8 m/s: offset 0.9 m and 1.3 m trip (truck left at 1.9 and 3.9 m/s); 1.7 m and 2.2 m do not (no trip, edge untouched, drives on at 8.8 m/s). The cut-off matches the hull circle (1.06) + rock radius (0.6) = 1.66 m; the 2.3 m mouldboard overhangs the circle by little, so a rock just outside it is passed.
- Blade lifted: no trip, edge stays 100.00 through a rock 12 m ahead at 9 m/s.
- Chain of two rocks and a log at 8 m/s with W held: one trip, one toast (the truck stops at the first rock). Edge 94.11, speeds finite.
- Edge at 3.8 m/s approach reads 97.03 only because W is held and the truck passes tripV before the rock; not a threshold bug.

1. POLISH — lifting the blade while it is folded over a rock: `down` false, `k` 0.00, but `trip` stays 0.43 and the mouldboard stays folded forward (`tk` 1.00) as it is raised; the riding hint is empty. Tapping the use control again re-lowers it onto the rock with the fold intact, no new trip, no edge loss. Harmless to numbers, but a lifted blade should unfold (or the fold should ride on `B.down`). Suggest: clear `B.trip` when the blade is lifted, or only hold the timer while `B.down`.
2. POLISH (note) — after any trip the truck is at about 0 m/s within 0.3 s even though the design says it keeps 60% of its speed: the hull circle then holds it against the rock (0231 saw the same). Fine as feel, but the toast/README "keeps 60%" never shows in play against a solid rock.
