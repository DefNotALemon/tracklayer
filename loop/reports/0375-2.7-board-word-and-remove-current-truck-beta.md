# 0375 — 2.7 follow-up (v295): board word beside a truck, and why `TLXV.remove` left one truck: beta

Scenario: new `x2_boardword_beta.mjs` (numbers only, no stills). Follows up the two open items in 0374.

## Findings
1. **OK: plain case.** Standing 2.5 m from an owned pickup on foot, `#wnHint` reads "Q: get in the pickup". `VEH.board` itself reads "get on the sled" at that moment, so the word is computed per spot, not stored.
2. **POLISH (unconfirmed which machine is meant): bogged branch still says "get on".** With `BOG.on` (forced by `enterBog()`) while standing beside the pickup, the hint is "E: dig her out (slow) · Q: get on" (tracklayer.js ~16705 hardcodes `"get on"`; v295 changed only the `footXUse` branch and `rigHint`). If the bogged thing is the sled, "get on" is right. If a truck can be bogged, it should use `VEH.board` as the other branches do. My forced bog was on the sled while I stood at the truck, so I could not tell. Suggest a one-line check of what `near` means in that branch.
3. **OK / explained: 0374's "cleanup leaves one truck" is by design, not a bug.** `removeVeh` (tlx-vehicles.js ~2103) refuses `s.cur`, a truck out with a driver, or one carrying a rig or load. On foot, `cur` is the nearest owned machine, and it stayed `v1` even after I teleported 400 m away and waited 1.2 s: `remove` -> `{n:0, left:1, cur:"v1"}`. So the last truck can never be cleared through `remove` while it is the only owned one. Matters only to scenarios and the mod menu's "Clear spawned machines" (the last spawned truck stays). Suggest the menu say so, or let `remove` clear `cur` when you are on foot beyond ~50 m.

Not covered: the "light the fire" and buried-car second-use lines (needs a fire or `TLXC` buried car set up beside the truck); the scenario's only fail is finding 2's assert, kept as a marker.
