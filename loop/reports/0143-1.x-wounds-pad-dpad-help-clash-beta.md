## Findings
1. [BUG, med] On a pad, D-pad down does not bandage you while a call for help is open. `helpWanted()` (empty tank, or the fjord) takes D-pad down first, so a rider who is bleeding with an empty tank presses the bandage button named on the bleeding bar and calls a $45 fuel delivery instead. Test: far backcountry, one fresh wound (age 1 s), `GS.fuel = 0`, D-pad down: wound still open (age 1.5 s, bandages 3), `HELP.on` and `called` true, toast "Fuel delivery is coming: about 70 s out". The bar at the bottom reads "D-pad down · 3 bandages" the whole time (still shows it). Same on the fjord. The wound window is only 6 s, so the player loses the bandage and the $45. Suggest: while a wound is still fresh (age under BANDAGE_WINDOW) D-pad down bandages first and a second press calls help (or give the help call its own pad button). Keyboard (U vs P) and touch (BANDAGE vs CALL HELP) don't clash.
2. [POLISH] Reloading the page is a free full heal. A wound is saved (x.sys.wounds holds age 3.1 and health 98.3), but the game saves no position, so the reload spawns at the depot (6 m from a site, fuel refilled to 16), where `tick` patches every wound and refills the bandages. Probe: bleeding 2.9 km from any site, reload, 0 wounds, hp 100. Cheap to use and it skips the $100 tow fee and the walk back; fine if intended, otherwise note it.
3. [POLISH] "Out of fuel. Press P for a fuel delivery" in the same still is a keyboard letter in a pad session (the bleeding bar beside it already goes through `btn()`). Probably not wounds' own, but it sits next to the clash above.

## Verified (no bug found)
- U on a fresh wound (age 1 s) dresses it; bandages 3 -> 2. A wound at age 7.8 s refuses ("soaked through"), bandage kept. With one soaked and one fresh wound open, U takes the fresh one.
- Touch: BANDAGE pill shows (88 px wide) while a wound is open, a pointerdown dresses it, and it hides again.
- U works with the tablet open. Bleeding continues behind the Settings screen (hp 99.7 -> 97.2 over about 6 s wall), consistent with the game not pausing.
- Bleed-out: 3 wounds with 6 hp left, 8 game seconds: tow blackout fired, cash 500 -> 400 (the $100 tow), health 100 and no wounds after, fuel refilled. (My first E assert checked `GS.dead` too late, after the respawn; the numbers show it worked.)
