[POLISH, med] **A raised wage can't be undone and has no cap or check.** 15 RAISE clicks in a row (any distance, no talk gate) took a $60 caretaker to $253, 6.1x the fair $41.6 for their real skill. Cash isn't checked and there is no confirm. Giving it leaves them at 0.6x fair, still Fed up (hap 35), and the next ask is a week away. Suggest: aim the ask at about 0.85x fair when pay is the cause, with the current +12-20 % as a floor.

LET GO fires at once with no confirm (the person is gone and the job is reposted). One mis-tap on touch costs a hire. Suggest: put LET GO last, or ask to confirm.

[POLISH, low] **Clock stepping back leaves the talk gate shut.** After a praise, GS.hour -30 h gives `hour - talk = -30`, so PRAISE and CRITICISE stay blocked ("You've talked to X today") until the clock has caught up.
