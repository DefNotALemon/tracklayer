- Sled riding, on foot beside a rolling truck, and garage open: no grit spent.

## Findings
1. [POLISH] Swapping away from the plow truck switches the spinner off. Swapping back finds it off (`grit: false`) although it was on when you left. Reasonable, but nothing tells the player; they may drive off thinking it still runs. Suggest a toast or hint line on re-entry ("gritter is off").
2. [POLISH] Reversing 2.5 s with the spinner on spends grit (3000 -> 2996 kg) like going forward. Probably fine; grit goes behind the truck's rear, which is the front when reversing. Worth a design decision.
3. [POLISH, scenario note] Section G of my scenario ran with no truck current (the sled), so "garage open while rolling in the truck" is only weakly covered. The hopper-spending path was not exercised on a real gritted road in this test (builder's x4_gritter covers it).

No BLOCKER or BUG found.
