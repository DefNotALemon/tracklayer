## Findings
1. OK — `seller` undefined: collected, one truck at the lay-by, cash finite, no throw.
2. OK — `paid` NaN: collected normally, cash stays finite (no refund path is hit).
3. OK — reload with a held truck whose `site` is "nowhere": loads, cash finite, held record kept, page errors none; Market app renders with every junk record.
4. POLISH — held record with `site` missing from SITES (deleted or "nowhere") or undefined: `mktCollect` matches `h.site === site.id`, so it can never be collected, and `heldRefund` only refunds when `MKTV.blocked(h)` is set, which it isn't. Paid cash ($1-2k tested, ask ~$16.8k+) is locked in the record forever. Only reachable with corrupt/old-schema data, so low impact. Suggested fix: in `heldRefund`, treat a veh record whose site isn't in `api.world.SITES` as blocked ("the yard is gone") and refund `h.paid`.
5. POLISH — `cond` NaN on the held record: the collected truck arrives at cond 0 (not rejected, not defaulted). Suggested: `Number.isFinite(h.cond) ? h.cond : 1` in `MKTV.collect`.
6. Note — a test-only fake site object `{id:"nowhere"}` handed to `mktCollect` collected a truck at the player's position; not a game path.
