# 0197 — 3.5b1 tractor unit, second beta: swap, hill, repair, car hit, reload — main @ f690fa4 (tlx-vehicles v19)
Scenarios (new): x3_tractor_swap_break.mjs, x3_tractor_hill_break.mjs. Numbers only, no stills. Page errors: none in either. Follows 0195 (which covered mount, fuel, night, licence reload).

## Findings
1. [POLISH] Repair is a flat $20 per condition point for every truck (`REPAIR_PT.truck`), so the $11,500 tractor unit costs the same to repair as the $4,200 pickup: 60 points down = $1,200 on both. The lorry is cheap to fix relative to its price (10% of price vs 29%). Suggest scaling by the vehicle's price (e.g. `price/350` per point) or a per-vehicle `repairPt`.
2. [POLISH] Hill pull, same 10 s of W from a standstill on the steepest route stretch (5.1%, plowed road, 200 L): tractor 60-62 m (14.3 m/s at the end), pickup 76-94 m (13.1 m/s). Slower off the line as designed, and it climbs without bogging. Only a 5% stretch exists on the routes, so steeper grades with the 7 t load are untested.
3. [POLISH] The pickup is bought with 70 L in a tank while the lorry arrives with a full 300 L. Presumably intended (as designed in the builder's asserts); noting it only because the pickup's range is short by comparison.
4. Holds: buying both (cash 60000 → 44300, both parked on the forecourt), 4 swaps tractor↔pickup↔tractor↔pickup land in the right vehicle with no throw and each keeps its own fuel (300 / 70), driving a tractor 18 m/s at a parked car throws nothing (speed drops to 0.8 m/s, no crash state), both vehicles survive a reload with the tractor still current and fuel 199.
5. [UNVERIFIED] Off-road 15% grade in powder: the tractor sat still (0.01 m/s, no bog flag) after 9 s of W. Expected for 7 t in fresh powder, but no "dig" / bogged hint was read in that probe; 0195 saw the hint on flat powder, so likely fine.

## Not covered
Trailers/fifth wheel (3.5b2 not landed), towing a sled with the lorry (no tow ball by design), pad/touch driving of the lorry uphill.
