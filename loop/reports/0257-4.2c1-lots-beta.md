## Findings
No BLOCKER.
1. BUG (exploit) — DROP then TAKE ON again wipes a lot's misses. With `miss = 1` set on the forecourt, `lotDrop` + `lotTake` leaves `miss = 0` (`lotTake` writes a fresh `{miss:0}`). So the "second miss and you lose it for a week" penalty is avoidable: DROP is free, drop a lot that is about to be dug out (or already has a miss) and retake it. Suggest keeping `miss` (and the lotOff gate) in a separate map, or have DROP of a lot with miss > 0 set a short cool-off.
2. POLISH — DROP also instantly re-plows a snowed-in lot (ground 0.5 -> 1.0, `s` 0), so dropping and retaking is a free full reset of the snow and the 12 h clock. Same fix as 1 covers the worst of it (retaking soon after a DROP could come back still snowed in).
