## Findings
1. [Verified, answers 0220 #1] Real driving counts the credit correctly. One keyboard drive up the stretch at 6-10 m/s with 0.15 m of snow marks the right-of-travel side for every length from the first metre to where I stopped (32/40 lengths, 0 on the other side). So the road credit works for a player. The 0220 puppet failure is a harness artifact: a puppet pass (teleported pose, 6 m/s fake velocity) marks lengths only from some way in. Per-length dump: out up the stretch it marks only the last 10 of 40 lengths (`0…0 | 0…01111111111`), back down it marks the first 32 and misses the last 8 of the travel; identical at lane offsets 0.8, 1.5, 2.2 and 3.0 m, so it is not the lane or the blade angle (a 0.49 rad). Cause in the harness not found (suspect `P.gnd` false or the near-route lookup lagging after a teleport); the builder's own section E passes because its passes are not run back to back. Do not read `x4_bladeroad_break.mjs` E1-E4 failures as game bugs; use the drive probe instead.
2. [POLISH] The pickup cannot start in a snowed-in stretch with the blade down at 0.35 m: from a standstill it is bogged inside half a second (speed 0, `bog` on, throttle held, never moves). At 0.15 m it pulls away and reaches 6 m/s in about 20 m. The a2 text says the pickup handles 55 cm "at 15 km/h", so a stretch left at 0.35 m can only be opened by someone who gets a run at it from the clear road beside it. If that is intended (it is realistic), the bog hint should say "back up and take a run at it"; suggested fix is a line, not physics.
3. [Verified] The side counted is the one on the player's right of travel; the credit needs both sides, so a second pass the other way is required, as designed. Not re-tested: the 80% threshold close-out toast, because the drive was stopped before the far end.

No BLOCKER or BUG. `x4_bladeroad_break.mjs` should be fixed or dropped by whoever owns it (it still exits 1 on main).
