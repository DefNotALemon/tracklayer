# 0124 — 2.3 pickup "a held truck says why" (v190, beta fix 0116 #1/#4) — main @ b47c573
Scenarios: `x2_revsilent.mjs` (builder's, rerun: all ok, page errors none) and new `tools/playtest/scenarios/x3_stillwhy_break.mjs` (tries to break it). No BLOCKER.

## Findings
1. [POLISH] W and S held together at a standstill on an open plowed lane: after 1.02 s the truck says "The wheels can't find grip here. Rock it back a little and try again." although it was moved 1.4 m and the player is simply braking and throttling. The "grip" branch is the catch-all. Suggest skipping the warning when both pedals are held (`input.brk > 0` with thrE > 0), or when the truck has moved any distance.
2. [POLISH] On a very steep hill (0.82 rise/run, forward from rest) the toast is "digging in", never "too steep to climb": `sinkS > 0.07` is tested before the grade, so any soft snow hides the steep reason. Fine for most hills; only matters where a grade is the real limit. Consider testing grade first when above ~0.3.
3. [POLISH, not confirmed as a bug] In the 8 s hold against the parked sled (test 2) and the 6 s hold against an obstacle (test 5) `R.why` was set at 1.5 s / 1.1 s but the toast list was empty when read at the end of the hold; the toast had presumably expired by then (the shorter holds do show it). Worth a glance that the "Something solid" toast stays long enough to read.
4. [OK] Single-fire per hold: release and press again against the sled warns again (1 toast each time); no repeat spam while held.
