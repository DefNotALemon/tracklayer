[BUG, low] The live cells under the truck don't follow the new rest depth quickly when a stretch is set to full: on fv888 at snow 1.0 the depth under the pickup read 0.23 → 0.24 → 0.25 m over 9 game s (rest is 0.86), and the pickup drove 8.6 m in 14 game s (maxv 5.6) versus 34 m at snow 0. So a fully drifted stretch, right after it goes full, is still easier to drive than a half drifted one until the cells fill. Real storms build snow gradually, so probably invisible in play; but a stretch that flips to 1 under a parked/moving truck (reload on a planted save, or the clock jump from sleeping) would load as full depth via the chunk path, so only already-loaded chunks lag. Suggest: when `snowApply` raises a stretch's snow, raise the loaded cells to `restCell` immediately, or log that refill rate is the intended ramp.

Impassable from about a third full means "storm of ~2 h closes the road" for trucks; matches the design text ("three hours leave about half a metre"), only noting it so the council plow timing (3.2a) is tuned against it.
