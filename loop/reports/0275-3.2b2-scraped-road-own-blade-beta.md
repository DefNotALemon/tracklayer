## Findings
1. [info] A snowed 400 m stretch (snow 0.5, January, clear) cut by `plowSeg(id, s, true)` scrapes at once (scrapeF 1.00); its two snowed neighbours stay 0. The scrape is per 400 m stretch, so the player's cut ends at an invisible seam, same as the council plow's.
2. [POLISH] A blade pass over a stretch with only a dusting (snow 0.01, below the 0.02 gate in `plowSeg`) does not refresh that stretch's plow time: scrapeF stays 0 on a road you just ploughed. Consistent with "no snow, nothing to cut", but a player who ploughs a lightly dusted road in a cold snap gets no scrape/spark feedback. Suggest: only stamp `t` when the stretch was actually below 0.02 AND the blade touched it, or leave as is.
3. [info] Reload keeps a half-plowed road: cut stretch still 1.00, snowed neighbour 0.00, no road error.
4. [info] Mild November day: the same fresh cut scrapes 0.16 (< 0.25), as designed.

[POLISH, test gap] My seam probe (scraped() along the line between two stretch centres) read 0 everywhere because the points are not on the road's wheel tracks, so the "no discontinuity at the seam" check is vacuous.
