## Findings (most important first)
1. [BUG] The wheel stays open under the tablet. Riding with the wheel open, Tab then T opens the tablet and `TLXWHEEL.W.open` is still true (x11_wheel_break2 step 5 fails: "tab true, wheel true"). `tlx-gunwheel.js` `modal()` includes `api.tablet()` only as a gate for OPENING; the tick's close condition is `!api.swapOk() || settings visible`, and `swapOk` (tracklayer.js ~17387) doesn't test the tablet. While both are up the wheel's capture-phase key listener swallows 1-9, Esc and R (`stopImmediatePropagation`), so the tablet can't see them. Suggested fix: add `|| api.tablet()` to the tick's close test (or to `swapOk`).
2. [POLISH] The wheel doesn't pause or slow the sled. Riding at ~33 m/s with W held and the wheel open, speed rose to 39 m/s over 1.5 game seconds with no input change. That's as designed ("pausing nothing"), but a rider can pick parts at 140 km/h. If Lemon wants it safer, close the wheel on a crash or throttle above some speed; it already closes on a crash (step 4 passes).
3. [OK] Holding R while riding opens the wheel without shifting gear (gear/dir 1/1 before and after). 3 picks the grip slot with no side effect. Esc closes only the wheel (no Settings, no garage). A crash closes the wheel and it won't open while down. After a reload the wheel is shut. Walking under an open wheel works (moved 1.56 m).
4. [OK] Rerun of x11_wheel.mjs on main: 44 ok, 0 FAIL, page errors: none. This covers keyboard, pad (tap doesn't open, hold does, A/B), touch (GUN button, tap/tap), shouldered rifle, the people-never-shot refusals, shop/wheel agreement and persistence.
