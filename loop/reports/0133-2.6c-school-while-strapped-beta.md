The follow-up 0105 listed as untested: start the Freight school (`schoolBegin`) while your sled is strapped to the deck trailer. Setup: truck + trailer bought, trailer free 11 m from the freight yard, sled strapped on it, app signed up, `SCHOOL.here` = freight.

## Findings
1. [BUG] The course starts but the school's Matriarch can't be driven. `schoolBegin` moves `P` to the yard start (about 10 m south of the school), but `strapTick` still sees `h.strap` and glues `P` back onto the trailer every frame. After 2.5 s of game time the school sled stood 8.1 m from the yard start and 0.7 m from the trailer; holding throttle for 2 s moved it 0.0 m. The only hint is the "sled is strapped to the trailer, unstrap it first" toast (fires on throttle), which makes no sense on the school's sled. A missions' plan (cones/route from the yard start) can't be run; a player has to quit the course, find the trailer and unstrap. Suggested fix: `schoolBegin` refuses while `TLXV.strapped()` ("Unstrap your sled from the trailer first"), or `strapTick` skips while `SCHOOL.on`. Refusing is simpler and also covers a trailer hitched to a truck.
2. [POLISH] Same state after `schoolEnd("quit")`: own sled `frontier` is back and still strapped, 0.7 m from the trailer (correct, nothing lost).

## Verified (no bug found)
- No NaN position/camera at any point; page errors none; quit restores the player's own sled and the strap.
