`x2_haulreverse.mjs` (committed): lower the ramp, hitch, drive the pickup off the lane, full lock both ways, brake to a stop, reverse
with full lock each way, fold angle, get out, unhitch, raise the ramp. 17 checks, 1 FAIL (finding 1). Page errors none.
Sheet: `~/.hermes/cache/scratch/tlloop/reports/0108.jpg`. No BLOCKER.

## What held
- Hitching with the ramp already DOWN works; the ramp stays down while towed; coupler stays 0.000 m off the ball through every check.
- Pickup + trailer at 11-14 m/s with full left then full right lock: coupler on the ball, no NaN, camera fine (unloaded pickup reaches
  20 m/s in the same 5 s, loaded 14: sensible drag).
- Trailer-to-truck angle after all of that: 0.18 rad, distance 6.7 m (no fold through the cab).
- Get out (wait for `DMT.on` to clear first), UNHITCH at the coupler, RAISE RAMP at the tail: all work.
- Off the plowed lane with full lock at speed the loaded pickup digs in ("The wheels are digging in. Ease off, or back out along your
  own ruts.") and bogs; the unloaded pickup does the same on the same route ("Stuck to the axles. Q: get out, then E to dig, or N to
  reset"). So bogging is the truck's own behaviour, not the trailer's.

## Findings
1. BUG (needs a look, not trailer-specific): after braking the pickup to a standstill on snow at (-1357, -319), holding S (with or
   without a steering key) for 8 s does not reverse it: speed stays 0.002 m/s, moved 0.00 m, `BOG.on` false, no toast. Same with the
   trailer left parked (NOHITCH=1). `rev` needs `input.brk > 0`, no throttle, `vf < 0.8`; one of those or `Fres > T0` (a loaded-up
   "stuck" that never reaches the bog) is holding it. The player sees a truck that ignores S with no hint. Suggest: rerun with
   `scenarios/x2_haulreverse.mjs` and log `rev`, `Fx`, `Fres`, `T0`; if it is deep snow, bog it or hint "STUCK".
2. POLISH: with the trailer hitched the digging-in toast and the later STUCK hint are two different messages for one situation; the
   first says "back out along your own ruts" but reverse (finding 1) did nothing.
3. POLISH (scenario hazard): `Q` out of the truck flags `FOOT.on` at once; the dismount (`DMT.on`) runs ~2 s more and E does nothing
   until it ends. Scenarios must wait for `!DMT.on`.
4. POLISH (repeat of 0095 #1): top-left line still reads "Frontier 340" while driving the pickup.

Not tested: pad/touch driving with the trailer, trailer on a steep slope or in water, two trucks near the coupler.
