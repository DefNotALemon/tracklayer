## Findings

   (NUM() turns garbage into 0). You'd get a free trailer in the sea/origin instead of "not owned". Suggest: reject when x/z aren't
   finite, or re-seat it on the forecourt.
2. POLISH: a saved trailer at an absurd position (x 1e9) loads and stays there, unreachable, with a Map pin off the map. Suggest
   clamping to the world half-size or re-seating on the forecourt (only reachable by a hand-edited save; low value).
3. POLISH (repeat of 0095 #1): the top-left HUD line still reads "Frontier 340" while sitting in the pickup towing the trailer
   (visible in the still). Name the vehicle you're in.
4. POLISH: in the still the ramp gate stands up as a large flat grey slab at the trailer's tail (ramp up). Reads like a tailgate
   panel, fine, but from behind it hides the whole deck; worth a glance when 2.6b adds the deck as a ride surface.
