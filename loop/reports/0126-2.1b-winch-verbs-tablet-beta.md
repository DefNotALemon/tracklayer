## Findings
1. [POLISH] Still open since 0098 #1, now also seen on winch: with the tablet open (Map app) on foot, E acts on the world behind it. Dig: `x2_usebreak` F still fails ("Digging 1% · E: stop"). Winch: `x2_winchuse_break` E, tablet open, hooked strap, E unhooks it (hooked -> out). Suggest `footUse()`/`footAction()` return null/no-op while `TABLET` is open, as for `GS.garageOpen`.
2. [POLISH] `footUse()` returns "HOOK" when a tree is within 2.6 m of the walker, but `footAction()` also needs the tree within line length of the fairlead. Sled 29 m from the tree (26 m line), walker 1.2 m from it: label HOOK, E fails with toast "The birch is out of reach of your 26 m line." The wnHint does say "Line's at its end (26 m). Hook what you can reach, or go back.", so the player is told; only the touch button/label promises something it can't do. Suggest `footUse()` apply the same `wd.len - 0.4` test and return null when out of reach.
