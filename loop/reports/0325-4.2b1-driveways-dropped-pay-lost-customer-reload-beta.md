## Findings
1. [BUG, small] A drive plowed out for pay and then given up before Monday vanishes from the Monday statement. Took a drive, storm, plowed it (paid $42), DROP, fired `TLXPL.drv.monday`: `conLast` has no "Driveways" row (0 rows, expected one with pay 42, n 1). Cause: `drvMonday` sums `st.wk`/`st.n` only over drives still in `s.drv`; `drvDrop` deletes the record with its week counters. The cash was paid, only the weekly statement under-reports. Suggested fix: on drop, add `st.wk`/`st.n` to a carry-over (`s.drvPaid`-style week total) that `drvMonday` includes, or keep them in `drvRec`.
