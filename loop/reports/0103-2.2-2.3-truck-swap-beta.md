# 0103: 2.2/2.3 two trucks, fuel/parcel swap, pad + touch get-off: beta test (main c2f3baf, v180)

Focus: slices 2.2/2.3 (fewest reports; nothing new landed since 0102). Covers what 0096 listed as untested. New scenario `x2_pickupswap.mjs` (committed): 15 asserts, all pass, page errors none. No BLOCKER, no BUG.

## What held
- Two trucks, each with its own tank and bed: truck A set to 33 L + 3 real parcels, boarding truck B gives B a full 70 L and an empty bed; going back to A restores 33.0 L and 3 parcels; B's 12 L is kept.
- Pad LB gets out of the truck; touch GET OFF (`tcQ`) gets out of the truck (button visible while seated).
- Save + reload: both tanks exact (32.99 / 12.00 L), A's 3 parcels still in the live bed.
- The sled keeps its own parked fuel through truck trips.

## Findings
1. POLISH: after touch GET OFF the on-foot hint reads just "tap GET ON", not which vehicle (same ambiguity as 0096 finding 4; worse with two trucks parked together).
2. POLISH: reload while standing on foot beside the truck puts you back seated in it (state after reload: `foot false`, in v1). Not wrong, but the player left the truck on purpose; a sled on foot may behave the same (not checked).
3. Note, by design: the garage forecourt tops up whichever vehicle is current for free (6 L/s), including while you stand on foot beside it. It tripped my first run (A went 33 -> 43 L). Parked trucks are not refilled; the tests move them 500 m out first.
4. Not tested: truck on ice, bog recovery of the truck, ramming a building, two trucks with big loads.

Scenario note: parcels in a test must have real `SITES` entries as `dest`/`from`; a plain-object `dest` crashes `updGameHud` (`j.dest.x`) and is dropped on save, so that is my fixture, not a game bug.
