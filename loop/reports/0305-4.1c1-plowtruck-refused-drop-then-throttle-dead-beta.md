# 0305 — 4.1c1 plow truck: a refused blade drop in 30 cm of uncut snow, then the truck won't move (G4, lane A) — main @ 4ced41a (tracklayer.js?v=255)
Scenario: tools/playtest/scenarios/x4_plowtruck_pad_cut_speed_beta.mjs (as committed it FAILs on finding 1; it was meant to test pad cutting at speed and never got that far). Throwaway probes in scratch only. No stills (nothing to see: the truck just sits).

Setup: fresh save, 90k cash + Freight licence, Nordtrekk Brøyt 4x2 bought and seated, flat strip with 30 cm of uncut powder (`__field`), noon, no weather, traffic off. The truck starts standing in that snow, sunk 0.253 m (P.y − surf).

## Findings
1. [BUG, high for anyone who tries it] Pressing the blade control (E, or L3 on a DualSense) while the wheels stand in uncut snow gets the right refusal ("your wheels stand in uncut snow it has not cut: back up to cleared ground") — and from then on the truck will not move. Held W on keyboard: v = 1e-6 m/s for 8 s of game time (z 0.000006 m). Held R2 on a pad: the same for 12+ s of game time, and a later fresh press does nothing. `why` is empty, no hint says anything beyond "E: drop the blade · 30 cm of snow ahead", no BOG warning, no crash. P.vz = 3 set from the console frees it at once (it then runs normally), so it is a stuck-at-rest state, not a physical wall.
   Control run: the same truck, same spot, same snow, no blade press, with W or R2: 0.6 m/s after 1.7 s, 2 m/s at 6 s, 5.6 m/s at 10 s, normal. Waiting 3.5 s idle first does not freeze it. So the blade press (the `use()` that is refused) is what leaves the truck held. Reproduced 4 of 4 times: L3 alone (no pad wake), L3 after a wake, keyboard E alone; the pad-wake alone (R2 at 0.01) is harmless.
   Suggested fix: look at what the refused drop in tlx-plow.js leaves set (a pending use-hold, `XL.hold/held`, `CJ`, or the 0116 "held truck" path in tlx-vehicles `R.why`): clear it on refusal, and if the truck is held for any reason, set `R.why` so the "truck sits still with the pedal held says why" line fires.
2. [POLISH] The refusal toast fits, but the standing hint ("E: drop the blade · 30 cm of snow ahead") still invites the same press that causes finding 1. Once fixed it is fine.
3. [POLISH, scenario note] Pad throttle reads R2 (button 7) at value; `padSet({7:0.01})` does not switch INP.dev to pad (needs pressed > 0.5), so a first press only "wakes" the scheme on the next frame.

## Verified since last time
