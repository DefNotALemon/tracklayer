# Tracklayer: store page options

Lemon's shortlist of 27 Nov: A, B and F (C winter town, D survival and E 71 North dropped), each now with a night capsule teasing the aurora.
Pick one (or a mix); the final assets (S3) are made for the pick.
Every line below describes something that is in the game today. Open `options.html` to see them as pages.

## A. The snow remembers
*The snow is the game.* For: Sim and physics players: SnowRunner, Lonely Mountains, anyone who stops to watch a tech clip.

**Short description** (191/300): A snowmobile courier game where every track you cut stays in the snow. Pack your own trails across Norway's far north, keep them open against the weather, and haul freight where no road goes.

**About this game**

Every metre of snow in Tracklayer is real: a height field you press down, carve into berms and crater on a hard landing. Fresh powder drags on the sled and drinks fuel. Snow you have already packed is fast and cheap to ride.

So the routes you keep open are the strategy. Calm weather fills a trail back in slowly. A storm takes it in under a minute.

- Deformable snow at 25 cm resolution, across eight kilometres of plateau, frozen lakes and birch valleys
- Your trails persist: ride them again faster, or lose them to the next snowfall
- Freight, rescue, aurora tours and plowing work, each with its own load and its own risk
- Sleds, engines and bolt-on parts that change how the machine sits in the snow
- Keyboard, any gamepad or touch, with prompts that match what you are holding

**Tags to lead with:** Simulation, Physics, Driving, Open World, Snow
**For it:** The one thing no other game on the store has. Easy to show in a ten-second clip.
**Against it:** Sells a mechanic, not a fantasy. Needs a trailer that shows a trail being cut and ridden again.

## B. Last courier north
*A working life on a sled.* For: Job-sim players: SnowRunner, Euro Truck Simulator, Death Stranding's delivery crowd.

**Short description** (193/300): Run freight off the coastal ferry and out across the Nordkinn plateau by snowmobile. Start with one small sled and a parcel. Work up to trucks, trailers, plow contracts and drivers of your own.

**About this game**

The ferry lands the freight at Kjøllefjord quay. Getting it to the villages, the herders' cabins, the wind farm and the lighthouse is your job, across snow that no road crosses.

Pay buys a better sled, then a pickup, then a lorry. The harder the country you can cross, the better the work you are offered.

- Deliveries, heavy haulage, search and rescue, aurora tours and armed courier runs
- A garage of sleds, engines and parts, plus a used market for the ones you can't afford new
- Pickups, a sled trailer, a lorry with semitrailers and a plow truck with a gritter
- Road contracts from the kommune, and staff who drive your trucks while you ride
- Fuel, cold, hunger and weather decide which jobs are worth taking today

**Tags to lead with:** Simulation, Driving, Open World, Automobile Sim, Singleplayer
**For it:** A proven audience that buys exactly this kind of game, and it describes most of what is already built.
**Against it:** Invites comparison with SnowRunner's graphics. The page has to lead with the snow, not the trucks.

## F. From one sled to a company
*Build a winter haulage business.* For: Tycoon and management players who want to drive the trucks themselves.

**Short description** (209/300): Start as a courier with one snowmobile. End up running the trucks, the plow contracts and the drivers that keep a peninsula in the Arctic supplied through winter. Every job can be done by you or by your staff.

**About this game**

The kommune needs its roads opened after every storm. The villages need freight. Households need their driveways cut without the snow ending up against the neighbour's door.

Sign for the work, buy the machines, hire the drivers. When one of them bogs a truck in a drift at night, go and tow them out yourself.

- Road, driveway and car-park plowing contracts with real snow to move and somewhere to put it
- Staff who drive your trucks on the actual roads, get stuck, and can be hailed or rescued
- A fleet: sleds, pickups, a lorry, semitrailers, a plow truck, boats
- Licences, upkeep, fuel and wear to pay for
- Flats, landlords and property in town

**Tags to lead with:** Simulation, Management, Driving, Open World, Economy
**For it:** Describes the deepest systems in the game (plowing and staff) honestly.
**Against it:** The business side has no balance pass yet, and tycoon players punish a loose economy.
