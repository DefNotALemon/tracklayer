About this game

Every metre of snow in Tracklayer is real: a height field you press down, carve into berms and crater on a hard landing. Fresh powder drags on the sled and drinks fuel. Snow you have already packed is fast and cheap to ride.

So the routes you keep open are the strategy. Calm weather fills a trail back in slowly. A storm takes it in under a minute.

  • Deformable snow at 25 cm resolution, across eight kilometres of plateau, frozen lakes and birch valleys
  • Your trails persist: ride them again faster, or lose them to the next snowfall
  • Freight, rescue, aurora tours and plowing work, each with its own load and its own risk
  • Sleds, engines and bolt-on parts that change how the machine sits in the snow
  • Keyboard, any gamepad or touch, with prompts that match what you are holding

Who it is for

Sim and physics players: SnowRunner, Lonely Mountains, anyone who stops to watch a tech clip.

For it

The one thing no other game on the store has. Easy to show in a ten-second clip.

Against it

Sells a mechanic, not a fantasy. Needs a trailer that shows a trail being cut and ridden again.

About this game

The ferry lands the freight at Kjøllefjord quay. Getting it to the villages, the herders' cabins, the wind farm and the lighthouse is your job, across snow that no road crosses.

Pay buys a better sled, then a pickup, then a lorry. The harder the country you can cross, the better the work you are offered.

  • Deliveries, heavy haulage, search and rescue, aurora tours and armed courier runs
  • A garage of sleds, engines and parts, plus a used market for the ones you can't afford new
  • Pickups, a sled trailer, a lorry with semitrailers and a plow truck with a gritter
  • Road contracts from the kommune, and staff who drive your trucks while you ride
  • Fuel, cold, hunger and weather decide which jobs are worth taking today

Who it is for

Job-sim players: SnowRunner, Euro Truck Simulator, Death Stranding's delivery crowd.

For it

A proven audience that buys exactly this kind of game, and it describes most of what is already built.

Against it

Invites comparison with SnowRunner's graphics. The page has to lead with the snow, not the trucks.

About this game

Kjøllefjord is a real place, and in Tracklayer every house in it holds a family: named people with trades, routines and opinions, who ski the trails, post on the local feed and ring in to Radio Nordkinn.

The work is delivering what the ferry brings. The rest is yours: ice fishing, cross-country skiing, a boat for summer, a flat with a landlord who may or may not fix the heating.

  • Households in every village, each with its own surnames, trades and habits
  • Gram, a local feed the town fills with pictures of what really happened that day
  • Six radio stations, call-ins included
  • Ice fishing, sea fishing, cross-country ski trails and a boat of your own
  • Polar night, the midnight sun and four seasons that change what the country lets you do

Who it is for

Cosy and life-sim players: Stardew Valley, Lake, A Short Hike.

For it

The biggest audience of the six, and the direction you are building toward.

Against it

Promises warmth the game only partly has yet: no romance or family yet. Risky before those land.

About this game

A new game starts in polar night. Out on the fell the wind fills your trail behind you, the visor frosts over and the fuel needle is the only clock that matters.

Go through the ice, bog the sled in powder or come off at speed and you will be on foot, bleeding, a long way from a light.

  • Warmth, hunger, fuel and wounds to manage, with clothing layers that matter
  • Storms that bury a packed trail in under a minute
  • Crashes with a body that tumbles, gets hurt and has to walk back
  • A winch, a tether and a rescue sled for when it goes wrong
  • Reindeer, moose, foxes and the occasional bear that is not pleased to see you

Who it is for

Survival players: The Long Dark above all.

For it

Strong mood, and the night and storm frames already look the part.

Against it

Survival fans expect crafting, shelter and permadeath. The game is gentler than this page suggests.

About this game

The kommune needs its roads opened after every storm. The villages need freight. Households need their driveways cut without the snow ending up against the neighbour's door.

Sign for the work, buy the machines, hire the drivers. When one of them bogs a truck in a drift at night, go and tow them out yourself.

  • Road, driveway and car-park plowing contracts with real snow to move and somewhere to put it
  • Staff who drive your trucks on the actual roads, get stuck, and can be hailed or rescued
  • A fleet: sleds, pickups, a lorry, semitrailers, a plow truck, boats
  • Licences, upkeep, fuel and wear to pay for
  • Flats, landlords and property in town

Who it is for

Tycoon and management players who want to drive the trucks themselves.

For it

Describes the deepest systems in the game (plowing and staff) honestly.

Against it

The business side has no balance pass yet, and tycoon players punish a loose economy.
